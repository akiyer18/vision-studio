# Live 10 Standard presets per genre (names verified against live-presets-inventory.md on this install)

Every name below exists in `knowledge/live-presets-inventory.md` (read from the running Live 10.1.43 Standard). `load_device {track, name}` resolves them case-insensitively, extension optional. Suite-only devices (Operator, Analog, Wavetable, Electric, Collision, Sampler, Echo, Ping Pong Delay) do NOT exist here; use Delay with Ping Pong on.

How to use (agent):
- Drum kits are Drum Racks: load on a MIDI track named "Drums" and write Drum Rack pitches (36 kick, 38 snare, 39 clap, 37 rim, 42 closed hat, 46 open hat, 45 low tom, 50 hi tom, 51 ride, 49 crash). Audition first: `load_device`, `play_for` 4 s, `judge_sound` or `listen_live`. Our Stability one-shots go into Simpler tracks; mixing a Core kit with 1-2 Stability layers is the fast path to a polished kit.
- Chord presets (E-Piano Cmin7 Chord, E-Piano CMaj7 Chord, ENOugh Chord Pad, Floot Aboot Chord, Afterfade Chord, Ion Snow Pad Chord, Clav Delaychord) play a WHOLE chord per key: write single notes (roots).
- Synth Rhythmic presets (Bass Groove, Space Arp, Felixian Plucks, The Deeper Blue, Jungle Out There, Euro Dance Lead ...) are tempo-synced arps/gates: hold one long note per bar or per chord.
- Effects presets (ENCOM Riser, F1 Riser, Hyper Riser, Vinyl Siren, Zipper Transport, Ominous Thud FX) are transitions: one note at the end of a section.
- MIDI effects available: Arpeggiator, Chord, Note Length, Pitch, Random, Scale, Velocity. Scale locks melodies to key; Velocity + Random humanize Drum Rack hats; Arpeggiator turns a pad into an arp.
