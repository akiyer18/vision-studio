# Tech House
slug: tech-house
aliases: tech house, techhouse, tech-house, fisher, chris lake, cloonee, michael bibi, hot creations, groovy house, bass house (adjacent)
tempo: 124-128

## Summary
Tech house, 125-128 BPM, swing 0.1 on hats/shakers. Drums: punchy tight kick (more click than deep house), sharp clap+snare layer on 2 and 4, offbeat open hat, 16th closed hats w/ accents, shaker/tambourine 16ths driving, perc hits (conga, rim, bongo) in sync'd spots, vocal chop as perc. Kick: "punchy tech house kick, tight click, 60 Hz body, 300 ms, compressed, dry". Clap: "sharp layered clap w/ tight room, bright, 160 ms". Pattern: kick 0,4,8,12:120 | clap 4,12:112 + snare layer | open hat 2,6,10,14:100 | closed 16ths 80-50-70-50 | shaker 16ths 55 accents on offbeats | rim 3,9,14:65 | conga 7:70 | vocal chop 11. Bass: rolling mono bass: short plucky saw/square or sub w/ filter, playing 16th-note offbeat rolls (steps 2,3,6,7,10,11,14,15 pattern variants) btw kicks, ducked hard, 1-2 pitches (root and b7/octave). Chords: minimal: one chord/key, a vocal hook, a synth stab or pluck riff; keys F, G, A minor. Tex: vocal chops/spoken phrases pitched, risers, white-noise sweeps, reverse hits, filtered breakdown. Form: 32 intro, 16 build, 32 drop, 16 breakdown, drop. Avoid: chords/pads filling the space (tech house is drums+bass+one hook), bass doubling the kick, no shaker.

## Era & tempo
- 2000s UK/Ibiza (Hot Creations, Toolroom), 2017-now peak (Fisher "Losing It", Chris Lake, Cloonee, Michael Bibi, John Summit). 125-128 BPM, machine-tight drums, rolling bass, one vocal hook, DJ structure.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 126 BPM, swing 0.1)
1. Rolling tech: kick 4otf 120 | clap 4,12:112 (+snare 4,12:90) | open hat 2,6,10,14:100 | closed 16ths 80-50-70-50 | shaker 16ths 55 with accents 2,6,10,14:75 | rim 3:65 9:65 14:65 | conga 7:70 (bar 2: 15:70) | vocal chop 11:85.
2. Minimal groovy (Bibi): kick 4otf | clap 4,12 | open hat 2,6,10,14:90 | closed hats only 1,5,9,13:45 | perc click 6,14 | shaker 8ths.
3. Bass-house lean (Chris Lake): kick 4otf | clap 4,12 + snare | open hat 2,6,10,14 | 16th hats 85-55 | tom fill 12-15 every 8 bars.
4. Breakdown: kick out, hats + shaker continue, vocal + riser 8-16 bars, kick returns on the drop with a crash.

## Bass
- TYPE: rolling mono bass: short decaying saw/square pluck (filter env), or a sub with a short envelope; heavy sidechain to the kick (the pump IS the groove). Register F1-C2. Pattern: 16th offbeat rolls between kicks: e.g. 2:100 3:85 6:100 7:85 10:100 11:85 14:100 15:85, mostly root with the b7 or octave on 14-15; occasional slide.
- Live (verified): Deep Wave Bass, Rump Shaker Bass, Tough Electro Bass, Bass Groove (tempo-synced, hold one note); Auto Filter lowpass with envelope; Compressor sidechain is not available via API: emulate with short bass notes that start after each kick.

## Harmony & keys
- Minimal: one key (Fm, Gm, Am), a single chord implied by the bass; a synth stab/pluck riff (2 notes), a pitched vocal hook, occasional organ stab on the drop. Avoid pads.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The vocal phrase (spoken/sung, pitched, chopped, repeated every 2-4 bars) is the hook; a 2-note synth riff; bass variation on the drop (octave jump); filter sweep into the drop.

## Textures & FX
- Risers (8-16 bars), white-noise downlifters, reverse cymbal, impact on the drop, vocal FX throws (delay), tape stop before the drop, filtered drums in the breakdown.

## Arrangement
- Intro 32 (kick, hats, perc) → 16 add bass → drop 32 (full groove + vocal) → breakdown 16 (no kick, vocal, riser) → drop 32 → outro 32. Fills every 8 (tom roll or clap roll on 12-15).

## Mix signature
- Kick and bass dominate and pump; claps bright; hats/shaker wide; vocal centered with delay; mono below 120 Hz.

## References (verify)
- Fisher "Losing It", Chris Lake "Turn Off the Lights", Cloonee "Sun Goes Down", Michael Bibi "Hanging Tree", Hot Since 82, Green Velvet "Bigger Than Prince".

## Common mistakes
- Pads and chords (fills the space the bass needs), bass doubling the kick on the downbeat, no shaker/16th movement, no vocal chop, soft or boomy kick, straight hats at one velocity, forgetting the 8-bar fill.
