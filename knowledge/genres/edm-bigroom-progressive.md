# EDM: Big Room / Progressive House (festival 2012-2016 and after)
slug: edm-bigroom-progressive
aliases: edm, big room, bigroom, big room house, festival house, progressive house, prog house, mainstage, festival edm, electro house, main room, swedish house, martin garrix style, avicii style
tempo: 126-130 (big room 128, electro house 128-130, melodic progressive 126-128)

## Summary
Big room/progressive house, 128 BPM, straight (swing 0). Drums: huge layered 909-based kick (sub + punch + click, 500 ms), snare/clap on 2 and 4 w/ a big reverb, 909 open hat on the offbeats, 16th closed hats in the build, snare rolls (8ths→16ths→32nds) into the drop, crash on the drop downbeat, white-noise sweeps. Kick: "big room festival kick, layered 909 punch and deep sub, hard click, 500 ms, saturated, dry". Clap: "huge EDM clap with a long bright reverb tail, 700 ms". Pattern (drop): kick 0,4,8,12:127 | clap 4,12:120 | open hat 2,6,10,14:110 | lead stab syncopated on 0, 3, 6, 10 | crash 0 bar 1. Bass: a sidechained saw/square bass playing the root in 8ths or syncopated w/ the lead (electro) or a sub following the lead's rhythm (big room); register C1-G2; ducks hard to the kick (short attack, 150-250 ms release). Chords: minor, 4-chord loops (i-VI-III-VII, vi-IV-I-V), supersaw chords in the break, the drop lead is a 1-2 bar minor-pentatonic riff w/ octave jumps on detuned saws + plucks. Tex: white-noise risers/downlifters, impacts, sub drops, vocal chops, reverse crash, snare roll. Form: intro 16/32 → break 16 → build 16 → drop 16/32 → break → build → drop → outro. Avoid: a weak kick, drops with too many elements, no sidechain pump, a drop melody that is a full chord progression instead of a riff, clap without reverb.

## Era & tempo
- Swedish House Mafia/Avicii/Alesso progressive 2010-2013: 126-128, big supersaw chords, melodic breaks. Big room 2012-2016 (Martin Garrix "Animals", Hardwell, W&W, Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike): 128, minimal drops w/ a lead stab riff and the kick, huge builds. Electro house (Zedd, Porter Robinson early, Knife Party): 128-130, gritty basses. 2017-now: future house / slap house 124-128 descend from this. Keys mostly minor (Am, Fm, Gm, Cm).

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 128 BPM, straight)
1. Big room drop: kick 0,4,8,12:127 | clap 4,12:120 | open hat 2,6,10,14:110 | lead stab 0:127 3:110 6:115 10:120 (bar 2: 0, 3, 6, 11) | crash 0 bar 1 | no closed hats (space for the kick).
2. Progressive drop: kick 4otf 124 | clap 4,12:115 | open hat offbeats 100 | closed 16ths 70-45 | bass 8ths on the "and"s sidechained | supersaw chords whole notes.
3. Electro house drop: kick 4otf 127 | snare+clap 4,12:120 | closed 16ths 85-55 | bass syncopated 0, 2, 5, 7, 10, 13 w/ the lead | fill: bass stutter bar 8.
4. Build (16 bars): kick 4otf → snare 8ths bars 1-8 → 16ths bars 9-12 → 32nds bars 13-16, pitch rising, white noise swell, highpass sweep on everything, 1 beat silence (or "one two three" shout) before the drop.

## Bass
- TYPE: big room = a sub (sine) following the lead stab rhythm or whole notes on the root; progressive = sidechained saw/square bass in 8ths (offbeats) or 16ths; electro = gritty FM/wavetable bass doubling the lead rhythm. Register C1-G2 (mono below 120 Hz). Sidechain: attack 0-5 ms, release 150-250 ms, ratio 4:1+ keyed from the kick (ghost kick in the break).
- Live (verified): Tough Electro Bass, Drop Buzz (electro), Analog Saw Bass, Boom Sub Bass (sub); sidechain = Compressor w/ Live's sidechain input from the kick track.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor keys (Am, Fm, Gm, Cm, Em): 4-chord loops i-VI-III-VII (Am-F-C-G), vi-IV-I-V in the relative major, i-VII-VI-VII; supersaw chord stacks (root-5th-octave + 3rd) in the break, piano/plucks introduce the melody, drop lead = minor pentatonic riff, 1-2 bars, octave jumps, syncopated.
- Instruments: supersaw (7-voice detuned), pluck (short saw w/ filter env), piano (break), big brass stab (big room), white noise, choir pad.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The drop riff (1-2 bars, repeated 8x, octave up on bar 9), the break melody (8-16 bars, piano/plucks then supersaw), the build's rising snare roll + pitch riser + "shout" sample, a 1-beat silence before the drop, a melodic callback in the second break.

## Textures & FX
- White-noise risers (8-16 bars) and downlifters, impacts/booms on the drop, sub drop, reverse crash into every section, vocal chops/shouts, sidechained noise pad in the break, filtered "radio" intro.

## Arrangement
- DJ intro 32 (kick, hats, bass hint) → break 16 (chords, melody) → build 16 (snare roll, riser) → drop 32 (riff, 16 bars then variation) → break 16 → build 16 → drop 32 → outro 32 (DJ-friendly, kick + hats). Phrases in 8/16/32; every 8 bars a fill (snare roll or reverse crash).

## Mix signature
- Kick and lead own the drop; bass ducks hard; clap drenched; mono low end below 120 Hz, wide supersaws above 200 Hz; loud, limited.

## References (verify)
- Swedish House Mafia "Don't You Worry Child", Avicii "Levels", Alesso "Years", Martin Garrix "Animals", Hardwell "Spaceman", DVLM & Garrix "Tremor", W&W "Bigfoot", Zedd "Clarity" (electro/prog), Knife Party "Bonfire".

## Common mistakes
- Thin kick, no sidechain, a drop that plays the chord progression instead of a riff, too many layers in the drop, dry clap, build without a roll/riser, breaks longer than 16 bars, random key changes.
