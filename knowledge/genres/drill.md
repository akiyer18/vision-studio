# Drill (UK / NY / Chicago)
slug: drill
aliases: drill, uk drill, ny drill, new york drill, chicago drill, brooklyn drill, drill beat, sliding 808, pop smoke type beat, 808 melo, central cee type beat
tempo: 138-150 (UK/NY, half-time); Chicago 60-70 (= 120-140 double)

## Summary
Drill, 140-146 BPM half-time. Drums: punchy short kick, hard snare/clap on beat 3 (and often a second on the "and" of 4 or displaced), hats w/ bouncing dotted-8th/16th skip ("3-against-4" feel), rolls into bars, perc clicks. Kick: "short punchy drill kick, hard click, 110 ms, dry". Snare: "cracking drill snare w/ clap layer and a short dark room, 220 ms". Signature pattern: kick 0:120 10:105 (bar2 0:120 6:92 11:105) | snare 8:127 + 14:100 (displaced second snare) | hats dotted skip 0,3,6,9,12,15 at 100/80 + 16th ghosts 55 | open hat 11 | perc click 4,12. Bass: sliding 808 (long, sat'd, legato glide), playing a melodic counter-line: hits the root w/ the kick on 0, slides up a 4th/5th on step 10-11 or down into bar 2; 2-4 notes per bar, slides lead INTO important notes, not constantly. Chords: dark minor (F, G, C# minor), violins/strings, piano, choir, bells, UK = eerie pads + plucked strings; NY = sample flips (Pop Smoke era) and bell leads. Tex: gun-cock/sword FX, reverse cymbals, vocal tags, risers. Form: 4-8 intro, 16 verse, 8 hook. Avoid: trap-straight hats (drill hats skip), 808 that only follows the kick, snare on 2 and 4, no second displaced snare, melody too bright/happy.

## Era & tempo
- Chicago drill 2011-13 (Young Chop): 60-70 BPM felt, trap drums, bleak. UK drill 2015-now (808 Melo, M1OnTheBeat, Ghosty): 138-146 BPM, sliding 808s, skippy hats, dark pianos/strings. NY/Brooklyn drill 2019-now (Pop Smoke, Fivio, AXL Beats): UK template + sample-based melodies, bigger 808 slides.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 142 BPM half-time)
1. UK drill core: kick 0:120 10:105 | bar2 0:120 6:92 11:105 | snare 8:127 and 14:100 (every bar, the second one is the drill tell) | hats dotted skip 0:100 3:80 6:100 9:80 12:100 15:80 + 16th ghosts at 55 on 2,5,8,11,14 (sparse) | open hat 11:85 bar 2,4 | perc click 4:60 12:60.
2. NY drill (Pop Smoke): kick 0:122 9:100 | snare 8:127 + 15:95 | hats 8ths 100/75 with 32nd flurry on 14-15 bar 4 | 808 big slide on 10→bar 2:0.
3. Chicago (half-time trap, 65 felt): kick 0:127 6:100 10:110 | snare 8:127 | hats 16ths 95-60 + triplet rolls | open 14.
4. Fill bar (every 4th): snare roll 12:80 13:90 14:100 15:110 + hats muted.

## Bass
- TYPE: sliding 808 (long decay, saturation, mono, legato glide 80-150 ms). In drill the 808 is a MELODY: root on 0 with the kick, slide to the 4th/5th/b7 on 10-11, or a slide down into the next bar's root; occasional quick double 16th before the snare. Never slide every note.
- Register: F1-C2. Live (verified): Sub808Bass, Distorted Sine Bass, Hip-Hop Sub Bass; or Simpler with a long 808 one-shot, Classic mode, legato glide; Saturator Drive 10-16 for the UK grit.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor keys (Fm, Gm, C#m, Dm), tempo-slow harmonic rhythm: 1 chord per bar or per 2 bars; i-VI, i-VII-VI, i-iv-v(harmonic minor leading tone for menace).
- Instruments: pizzicato/legato strings, dark piano (felt/hall), choir "ooh", bells, eerie pads, plucked guitar (UK), sampled soul/pop flips pitched down (NY), flute.
- Melody: 2-bar motif, often a descending minor line with a raised 7th for tension; sparse, lots of space for the 808 slides.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The 808 slide is the hook; a counter-melody (bell/flute) enters on the hook; strings swell into the drop; vocal tag before the beat drops; melody drops out on bar 1 of the hook while the 808 slides.

## Textures & FX
- Gun-cock/sword/whoosh FX on fills, reverse cymbal and riser before the hook, vocal "grr/baow" tags, rain/vinyl ambience under intros, sub drop into the first bar.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4-8 (melody + tag) → verse 16 → hook 8 → verse 16 → hook 8 → outro 4. Drop the hats for bar 1 of the hook; 808 slide and FX mark every 8 bars; fill every 4.

## Mix signature
- 808 and snare dominate; kick short and clicky; hats panned slightly, melody dark and reverbed. Mono sub, saturated.

## References (verify)
- Pop Smoke "Dior"/"Welcome to the Party" (808 Melo), Headie One "Know Better", Central Cee "Doja", Fivio Foreign "Big Drip", Chief Keef "I Don't Like" (Chicago).

## Common mistakes
- Straight trap hats (drill skips), snare only on 3 (drill wants the displaced second hit), 808 glued to the kick with no slides or melody, happy/bright melodies, kick with sub fighting the 808, no fills every 4 bars.
