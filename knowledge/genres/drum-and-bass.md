# Drum & Bass / Jungle
slug: drum-and-bass
aliases: drum and bass, drum & bass, dnb, d&b, jungle, liquid dnb, neurofunk, jump up, ragga jungle, amen break, reese bass, 174
tempo: 170-176 (jungle 160-170 in the 90s, modern 172-175)

## Summary
Drum & bass, 172-175 BPM. Two-step drums: hard kick on 1 and the "and" of 3 (steps 0 and 10), snare on 2 and 4 (steps 4 and 12) layered w/ clap or a break snare, 16th/8th hats, ghost snares, and (jungle) a chopped Amen/Think break underneath w/ its own ghost hits. Kick: "tight punchy drum and bass kick, hard click, short 150 ms, dry". Snare: "cracking DnB snare layered w/ break snare and a clap, sharp 3 kHz crack, 160 ms, bright, compressed". Pattern (two-step): kick 0:125 10:115 | snare 4:127 12:127 | hats 8ths 95/65 or 16ths 80-50 | ghost snare 7:45 14:50 | ride on drops | break layer chopped. Bass: Reese (two detuned saws, lowpass, moving filter) or sub sine following it an octave down; liquid = sub + warm synth; jump-up = resonant wobble; neuro = heavily processed modulated bass. Pattern: long notes on the downbeat or an 8th-note offbeat bounce (steps 2,6,10,14), bass answers the kick, 1-2 notes per bar w/ slide into bar 2. Form: 32 intro, 16 build, 32 drop, 32 breakdown, drop 2. Avoid: snare on every beat (it's 2 and 4), no ghost notes/break layer, bass without movement.

## Era & tempo
- Jungle 1992-95 (Goldie, Shy FX, Dillinja): 160-170, Amen/Think breaks, ragga, Reese. DnB 1996-now: liquid (Calibre, High Contrast, Netsky) warm/melodic; neurofunk (Noisia, Mefjus) heavy sound design; jump-up (Hazard, Macky Gee) resonant wobble bass; 172-175 BPM standard.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 174 BPM, straight; break layer provides the swing)
1. Two-step (modern): kick 0:125 10:115 | snare 4:127 12:127 | hats 8ths 95/65 (or 16ths 80-50) | ghost snare 7:45 14:50 | ride 8ths on drops.
2. Jungle (Amen chop): programmed kick 0,10 + snare 4,12 ON TOP of a chopped Amen: chops reordered every bar, snare fill 13-15 bar 4, "ghost" Amen hats constant; pitch the break -2 to +3 semitones per bar for variation.
3. Liquid roller: kick 0:120 10:110 (bar2 adds 6:85) | snare 4:125 12:125 | 16th shaker 40 | hats 8ths 90/60 | rim 3:55.
4. Jump-up: kick 0:127 10:120 | snare 4:127 12:127 | open hat 6,14:95 | bass wobble hits with the kick.

## Bass
- TYPE: Reese (two detuned saws ±20-60 cents, lowpass ~600 Hz with slow movement, mono, sine sub an octave below) for jungle/neuro; warm sub + soft synth for liquid; resonant wobble (LFO on filter, 1/8-1/4 sync) for jump-up. Register F1-C2 (sub) with mids above.
- Pattern: long notes on 0 (hold 2-4 beats) with a slide into bar 2; or 8th-note bounce on 2,6,10,14 (jump-up: hits with the kick on 0 and 10); 1-3 notes per bar; movement comes from the filter, not note count.
- Live (verified): reese-like: Fat Bass Wobble, Wobble 2 Bass; acid/neuro: Reso Blaster; sub: Boom Sub Bass, Deep Wave Bass; Auto Filter lowpass with LFO (1/8 for wobble); Chorus for width on the mids layer only.

## Harmony & keys
- Liquid: m7/m9/maj7 pads and Rhodes, i-VI-VII (Fm-Db-Eb), ii-V-i, lush strings, piano; jungle: ragga/soul vocal samples, sparse chords; neuro: atonal, single note stabs. Keys Fm, Gm, Dm, Em.
- Instruments: Rhodes, pads (lush), strings, piano, vocal samples (ragga MC chats, soul snippets), FM/wavetable stabs, atmospheres.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- Vocal sample phrase on the intro/drop; the bass motif (2-bar) is the hook; a pad progression that opens on the drop; snare roll + riser into the drop; drop-out for 1 bar before the drop (the "drop silence").

## Textures & FX
- Risers (16 bars), reverse snares/cymbals, Amen stutters, ragga vocal snippets, atmospheric pads, vinyl crackle (liquid), impact + sub drop on the drop, delay throws.

## Arrangement
- Intro 32 (atmos, break hints) → build 16 (snare roll, riser) → drop 32 (full) → 32 variation (bass switch) → breakdown 32 (pads, vocal) → build 16 → drop 2 (64) → outro 32. Fills every 8 (snare roll 12-15 or Amen stutter).

## Mix signature
- Snare loudest and brightest, kick short, bass huge and mono below 100 Hz with wide mids, pads behind, break layer dusty and compressed.

## References (verify)
- Goldie "Inner City Life", Shy FX "Original Nuttah", Dillinja "The Angels Fell", Calibre "Even If", High Contrast "Return of Forever", Netsky, Noisia "Stigma", Hazard "Bricks Don't Roll".

## Common mistakes
- Snare on every beat or on 1 and 3; no ghost notes or break layer (sounds like a fast 4-piece kit); static bass with no filter movement; boomy kick masking the sub; tempo below 168; pads too loud over the drop.
