# Afrobeats (modern Nigerian/Ghanaian pop)
slug: afrobeats
aliases: afrobeats, afrobeat (modern), afro pop, afropop, naija, burna boy, wizkid, rema, afro fusion, afroswing, amapiano (adjacent), afro house (adjacent)
tempo: 98-112 (afroswing 100-105, amapiano 112-115)

## Summary
Afrobeats, 100-108 BPM, swing 0.15-0.25 (laid-back), perc-led. Drums: soft round kick w/ sync'd pattern (not four-on-the-floor), rimshot/snap or clap on the backbeat often pushed off-grid, const. 16th shaker, congas/talking-drum or log-drum (amapiano) accents, wood perc, clave-like patterns. Kick: "soft round afrobeats kick, warm low thump, soft attack, 300 ms, dry". Rim: "wooden rimshot/snap, bright click, 120 ms, dry". Pattern: kick 0:115 3:95 6:105 10:110 13:85 | rim/snap 4:110 12:110 (slightly late) | shaker 16ths 55 accents 2,6,10,14 | conga 7:85 15:75 | log drum (amapiano) 2:100 7:95 11:90 14:85 | clave 3,6,10,13 (soft). Bass: warm synth/electric bass, melodic and sync'd: short notes on 0, 3, 6, 10 w/ slide, following the kick's syncopation, octave pops; or amapiano log-drum bass carrying the groove. Chords: major and relative minor, 2-4 chords (I-V-vi-IV, vi-IV-I-V), guitar plucks (clean, highlife-style arpeggios), warm pads, marimba/kalimba, piano, brass stabs; keys G, A, C major. Tex: vocal chops/ad-libs ("ehh", "yeah"), whistle, bird/ambience, reverse FX. Form: 4-8 intro (guitar/perc), 16 verse, 8 hook w/ full perc. Avoid: 4otf kick, trap hats, no shaker.

## Era & tempo
- 2010s-now Lagos/Accra/London (Wizkid, Burna Boy, Davido, Rema, Tems; producers Sarz, P2J, Kel-P, Pheelz). 98-112 BPM, swung 16ths, percussion ensembles (shaker, conga, rim, log drum from amapiano since 2020), highlife guitar, warm synth bass. Afroswing (UK) adds trap-leaning drums at 100-105.

## Drum sounds (Stable Audio-ready)
- Kick: "TrackType: SFX, soft round afrobeats kick drum, warm low thump, soft attack, 300 ms decay, dry one-shot"
- Rim/snap: "TrackType: SFX, wooden rimshot with a finger-snap layer, bright click, 120 ms, dry"; clap alt "soft layered clap, 150 ms"
- Shaker: "TrackType: SFX, soft shaker, 16th-note feel, 80 ms, dry"
- Conga: "TrackType: SFX, conga open tone, warm skin, 250 ms"; slap "conga slap, bright, 120 ms"
- Log drum: "TrackType: SFX, amapiano log drum, deep woody tuned bass hit, 350 ms, rounded, dry"
- Perc: woodblock, clave, cowbell (low), talking drum glide, triangle.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 104 BPM, swing 0.2; rim 10-20 ms late)
1. Modern afrobeats: kick 0:115 3:95 6:105 10:110 13:85 | rim/snap 4:110 12:110 | shaker 16ths 55 (accents 2,6,10,14:75) | conga 7:85 15:75 (bar 2: 1:70 9:80) | clave 3:50 6:50 10:50 13:50 | open hat 14:70 bar 4.
2. Amapiano-leaning (112): kick 0:110 8:105 (sparse) | shaker 16ths 60 | log drum 2:100 7:95 11:90 14:85 (the bass!) | rim 4,12:100 | perc fill 12-15 every 4.
3. Afroswing (102): kick 0:120 7:100 10:105 | snare/snap 4,12:115 | hats 8ths 90/60 w/ 16th skips | conga 6,14.
4. Breakdown: kick out, shaker + conga + guitar continue 8 bars, vocal ad-libs.

## Bass
- TYPE: warm synth bass (soft saw/triangle, short release) or clean electric; amapiano uses the log drum as the bass. Register G1-D2. Pattern: melodic and syncopated, following the kick: 0:100 3:85 6:90 10:95 with a slide into the next root, octave pop on 14; 3-5 notes per bar, playing the chord roots and 5ths; never a static root.
- Live (verified): Analogue Bass, Mello Bass, Fine Line Bass; log drum = Simpler on a tuned woody one-shot (or Percussion Core Kit for the hand percussion layer).

## Harmony & keys
- Major/relative minor, 2-4 chords: I-V-vi-IV (G-D-Em-C), vi-IV-I-V, I-IV-V, often with add9/sus; 1 chord per bar or per 2 beats in the hook.
- Instruments: clean electric guitar plucks (highlife arpeggios, 8th/16th patterns, slight chorus), warm pads, marimba/kalimba, piano, muted brass stabs, flute, synth plucks, strings (light).
- Voicings: guitar in the mid-high register playing arpeggios; pads low and soft; marimba doubling the melody.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- A guitar/marimba riff (2 bars) that answers the vocal; vocal ad-lib chops ("ehh", "oh na na") as a hook; a whistle melody; call-and-response between guitar and bass; percussion drop-out on the first bar of the hook then full ensemble.

## Textures & FX
- Vocal chops/ad-libs, whistles, birds/beach ambience, reverse cymbal, short risers, wood perc fills, tape stop before the hook, delay throws on vocal chops.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4-8 (guitar riff + perc) → verse 16 (kick, bass, shaker) → pre 4 → hook 8 (full perc, log drum, ad-libs) → verse 16 → hook 8 → bridge 8 (perc + vocal) → hook 8 → outro 8. Fills every 4 (conga/perc flurry on 12-15).

## Mix signature
- Percussion wide and present, kick soft and round, bass warm and loud, guitar bright and panned, vocal chops wet; little low-mid clutter.

## References (verify)
- Wizkid "Essence", Burna Boy "Last Last"/"Ye", Rema "Calm Down", Davido "Fall", Tems "Free Mind", Asake (amapiano-leaning), Fireboy DML.

## Common mistakes
- Four-on-the-floor kick; trap hats; no shaker/conga layer; straight grid without the lazy swing; static root bass; EDM-style drops; log drum used as a constant layer without space.
