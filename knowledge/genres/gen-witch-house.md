# Witch House
slug: gen-witch-house
aliases: witch house, drag, haunted house, occult electronic, ghost drone, screwed synth, dark wave trap, witchy electronic, salem style, crunk drag, slowed occult, tri angle sound, goth trap
tempo: 60-80 halftime feel (often programmed at 120-160 double-time; drag/screwed 55-70)

## Summary
Witch house, 60-80 BPM halftime (often programmed at 130-150), straight or light swing 0.05-0.1. Drums: slowed 808 kick w/ long boom, crushed snare/clap w/ huge dark reverb on 3 (halftime), sparse 8th hats w/ trap rolls, pitched-down perc, tambourine, reverse cymbals. Kick: "slowed 808 kick, 45 Hz, 900 ms, bitcrushed". Pattern (16 steps, halftime): kick 0,7,10:120 | snare 8:110 | hats 8ths 70-50 w/ 32nd roll 14-15 | reverse cymbal into bar 1. Bass: long 808 sub w/ pitch glides on root/b2/b5, sustained and saturated, or a droning detuned saw bass. Harmony: minor/phrygian 2-chord loops (i-bII, i-bVI), detuned analog pads, pitched-down choirs, music-box/organ leads. Tex: chopped and screwed vocals slowed -5 to -12 st, tape hiss, VHS warble, bitcrush, cavernous reverb. Form: 8-bar loop blocks, slow fade-ins, element dropouts, 3-4 min. Avoid: a clean hi-fi mix, fast/EDM energy w/ build-drops, bright major chords or clear upfront vocals.

## Era & tempo
- Core wave around 2009-2012: Salem, oOoOO, White Ring, Balam Acab, Holy Other, Mater Suspiria Vision, Gr†ll Gr†ll, Ritualz. Associated labels include Tri Angle and Disaro.
- Roots: Houston chopped and screwed hip-hop (DJ Screw), Southern rap 808s, shoegaze, darkwave/goth, drone, industrial.
- Tempo: the halftime feel sits at 60-80 BPM, with drag tracks at 55-70.
- Later echoes: the dark trap, phonk and "witch" aesthetics of the 2010s-2020s, which run faster (130-150 with a halftime feel).

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 70 BPM halftime feel, straight)
1. Drag classic: kick 0:120, 7:100, 10:110 | snare 8:110 (huge reverb) | hats 8ths 70-50 | 32nd hat roll 14-15 bar 4 | reverse cymbal 12-15 bar 8.
2. Trap-gothic: kick 0, 3, 10:120 (808 w/ glide) | clap+snare 8:115 | hats 16ths 60-40 with triplet rolls bars 2/4 | open hat 6:70 | crash 0 bar 1.
3. Sparse ritual: kick 0:110 every bar | snare 8:100 every other bar | no hats; tambourine 4, 12:60 | gong/bell 0 bar 1 | drone constant.
4. Dropout: drums cut for 4-8 bars (pad, vocal and hiss only), then return on the downbeat with a reverse-reverb swell. No snare roll.

## Bass
- TYPE:
  - (a) Long 808 sub (sine with saturation), 1-2 notes per bar, slow pitch glides (root to b2, or root up to b5), sustained over the kick.
  - (b) A droning detuned saw/square bass holding the root under the pads.
  - (c) A distorted, low-passed synth bass doubling the chord root.
- Register: E1-C2. Movement is slow and heavy, with no busy lines.
- Live: Operator or Simpler with a sine 808 sample, glide on and pitch envelope; Saturator/Roar for grit; Auto Filter lowpass.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor and phrygian (b2 for dread). Common loops:
  - 2-chord drones: i-bII, i-bVI, i-iv.
  - Suspended or open fifths.
  - Diminished passing tones.
- Slow chord rhythm: 1 chord per 1-2 bars.
- Keys: C#m, Ebm, F#m, Gm (often the result of pitching samples down).
- Instruments: detuned analog-style pads (chorus, slow attack), pitched-down choirs/"ooh" vocals, church organ, music box, celesta, eerie bells, cheap 90s rompler strings, sampled pop/R&B chopped and slowed.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- Screwed vocal chops (pitched down, time-stretched, stuttered).
- A 3-4 note music-box or bell motif.
- A choir swell on bar 1.
- A whispered or chanted phrase.
- Reverse-reverb vocal into the downbeat.
- An occult-style lead with heavy vibrato.
- An 808 glide as the hook.

## Textures & FX
- Tape hiss and wow/flutter, VHS warble, vinyl crackle.
- Bitcrush/downsampling (Redux, Erosion).
- Huge dark plate/hall reverb (Hybrid Reverb), Grain Delay shimmer, reverse reverbs.
- Field noise (rain, wind, church ambience), drones, low rumbles.
- Sidechained pad pumping (subtle).
- Pitch-dropped samples at -5 to -12 semitones.

## Arrangement
- Loop-based 8-bar blocks. A typical structure:
  1. Intro 8-16: drone, hiss and vocal fragment.
  2. Beat in: 16 bars.
  3. Variation: 16 bars, adding a choir/lead.
  4. Breakdown: 8 bars, drums out.
  5. Return: 16 bars.
  6. Outro: fade into hiss.
- Total 3-4 min. Changes come from adding and removing elements, not from builds.

## Mix signature
- Murky and lo-fi but heavy in the low end: 808 and kick dominant.
- Vocals buried and drenched in reverb.
- Highs rolled off (lowpass around 8-10 kHz on the bus), saturated and slightly distorted.
- Wide washed pads, mono sub.
- Deliberately degraded, but bass still controlled.

## Common mistakes
- Mix too clean/bright and modern.
- Tempo or energy too fast; EDM build-drops and snare rolls.
- Major or happy chords.
- Clear, upfront dry vocals.
- 808 without saturation or glide.
- Too many busy hats.
- Reverb so long it swamps the low end.
- No pitch-down / screwed treatment.
