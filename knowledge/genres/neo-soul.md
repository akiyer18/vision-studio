# Neo-Soul
slug: neo-soul
aliases: neo soul, neo-soul, neosoul, r&b soul, d'angelo, erykah badu, soulquarians, questlove drums, drunk drums, modern soul, rnb soul beat
tempo: 68-96

## Summary
Neo-soul, 70-92 BPM, the "drunk" pocket: kick on the grid, snare 20-40 ms late, hats loose, swing 0.3-0.45. Drums are live-sounding and dry: fat round kick, deep cracking snare (often a layered rim), brushy hats, ghost notes everywhere. Kick: "TrackType: SFX, fat round live kick, soft beater knock, 400 ms, close-miked, dry". Snare: "deep cracking snare with rimshot, wood and wire, 350 ms, dry studio". Pattern: kick 0:115 7:90 10:105 | snare 4:118 12:118 late + ghosts 2:40 7:45 11:42 15:38 | hats 8ths 90/55 w/ 16th ghosts 35 | rim 9:60. Bass: TYPE = fingered electric (Pino Palladino) or Moog: melodic, syncopated, behind the beat, plays around the kick, 3-6 notes per bar with slides and dead notes, octave jumps. Harmony: extended: m9, m11, maj9, 13ths, slash chords, chromatic passing; Rhodes (phaser/tremolo), Wurli, clavinet, strings, horns, gospel voicings. Textures: vinyl, vocal ad-libs/harmonies, tape, tambourine, congas. Form: 4 intro, 8/16 verse, 8 chorus, bridge with a key change or vamp. Mistakes: quantized drums, simple triads, kick-locked bass, clean drum machine sounds, no ghost notes.

## Era & tempo
- 1995-now: D'Angelo, Erykah Badu, Maxwell, The Roots, Jill Scott; Soulquarians (Questlove, Dilla, James Poyser, Pino Palladino). 68-96 BPM. The identity is the pocket: elements deliberately out of time with each other, warm live instruments, gospel/jazz harmony.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 84 BPM, swing 0.35; snare late by 20-40 ms, hats late 10 ms, kick on grid)
1. Drunk pocket (Voodoo): kick 0:115 7:90 10:105 (bar2 adds 13:75) | snare 4:118 12:118 late | ghosts 2:40 7:45 11:42 15:38 | hats 8ths 90/55 + 16th ghosts 35 | rim 9:60.
2. Gospel shuffle (90-96, swing 0.45): kick 0:118 6:95 8:100 11:90 | snare 4:120 12:120 | hats swung 16ths 85-50-70-45 | open hat 14.
3. Ballad (70, half-time feel): kick 0:110 8:95 | snare 8:118 (cross-stick on 4,12 at 60) | hats 8ths 80/50 | tambourine 8.
4. Breakdown: drums to rim + hats only, bass walks.

## Bass
- TYPE: fingered electric bass (round, slightly muted, Pino-style behind the beat) or Moog-ish synth bass for the funkier side. Register E1-G2. Pattern: melodic and syncopated: root on 0 slightly late, 16th pickups into the kick, slides between roots, dead notes (ghost thumps), octave jumps on the "and" of 4; 3-6 notes per bar, avoids doubling every kick.
- Live (verified): Fine Line Bass, Mello Bass, Electric Bass Drive; Moog type: Smoked Analog Bass, Dank Moug.

## Harmony & keys
- Extended jazz/gospel harmony: Ebm9, Abm11, Dbmaj9, Bbm7(b5), slash chords (F/G), chromatic approach chords, ii-V with alterations, modal vamps on one m11 chord. Keys with flats common (Eb, Ab, Db, Bb minor).
- Voicings: rootless Rhodes voicings (3-7-9-11), left hand roots sparse, right hand clusters; strings pads; horn stabs; clavinet comping; organ swells.
- Instruments: Rhodes (tremolo/phaser, slight dirt), Wurlitzer, grand piano, clavinet, Hammond, strings, horns, nylon/clean guitar with wah.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- A repeating Rhodes figure, a bass riff as the hook, vocal harmony stacks (ooh/aah), horn line answering the vocal, a chord change into the bridge (modulation up a half step or relative major).

## Textures & FX
- Vinyl crackle (light), tape saturation, vocal ad-libs, tambourine on 2 and 4, congas, string swells, reverse Rhodes, phaser on keys.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4 (Rhodes vamp) → verse 16 → pre 4 → chorus 8 → verse 16 → chorus 8 → bridge 8 (new chords/key) → chorus 8 → outro vamp 8+. Dynamics via instrumentation, not drops.

## Mix signature
- Warm, drums dry and present, bass big and round, Rhodes wide; vocals (if any) with plate reverb. Low end warm not sub-heavy.

## References (verify)
- D'Angelo "Voodoo" (Untitled, Spanish Joint, Devil's Pie), Erykah Badu "Mama's Gun", Maxwell, The Roots "Things Fall Apart", Jill Scott, Hiatus Kaiyote, Tom Misch, Robert Glasper.

## Common mistakes
- Quantized drums, triads instead of extended voicings, bass glued to the kick, drum machine sounds, no ghost notes, straight hats, over-busy drums, too much reverb.
