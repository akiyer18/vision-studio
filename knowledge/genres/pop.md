# Pop (modern pop production, 2015-2025)
slug: pop
aliases: pop, pop song, pop beat, pop production, modern pop, radio pop, top 40, dance pop, electropop, synth pop, bedroom pop, pop track, max martin style, dua lipa style, the weeknd style
tempo: 96-124 (ballads 70-90, dance pop 115-124, mid-tempo 100-110)

## Summary
Modern pop, 100-120 BPM, swing 0-0.12. Drums: hybrid: tight punchy kick (short, 60-80 Hz knock) often doubled by an 808 on roots, snare = clap + snap + a short snare layer on 2 and 4, crisp closed hats in 8ths w/ 16th fills, pre-chorus hat 16ths, chorus adds a wide clap/tambourine and a crash; dance pop = four-on-the-floor kick + offbeat open hat. Kick: "tight modern pop kick, short punchy knock at 70 Hz, clean click, 250 ms, dry". Snare: "pop clap layered with a snap and a short bright snare, sharp, small room, 200 ms". Pattern (mid-tempo): kick 0:118 6:95 10:110 | clap+snap 4:122 12:122 | hats 8ths 85/55, 16ths at 14-15 | tambourine 2,6,10,14:60 (chorus). Bass: synth bass or 808 on roots (long notes, octave pops), or a plucky picked bass (indie/bedroom), register E1-E2, locks to the kick and holds through the chorus. Chords: diatonic 4-chord loops (vi-IV-I-V, I-V-vi-IV, I-vi-IV-V, IV-I-V-vi), major w/ a sad vi start, borrowed minor iv for lift, keys C, G, D, A, F, Bb, Eb; piano/pluck/pad voicings w/ a top-note hook. Hook: topline melody + a signature synth/pluck riff (2 bars), vocal chops as instruments, a post-chorus "drop" w/ the riff. Tex: risers, reverse snare, filtered intro, vocal chop stabs, white noise swells, tambourine, finger snaps. Form: intro 4-8, verse 8-16, pre 8, chorus 8-16, post 8, verse 8, pre 8, chorus, bridge 8, chorus x2. Avoid: muddy low end (kick vs 808), dull snare, no pre-chorus lift, 5+ minute forms, a chorus that doesn't open up (more layers, wider, higher).

## Era & tempo
- 2015-2018: tropical/dance pop 100-120 (Bieber "Sorry", Dua Lipa "New Rules"), trap-influenced pop 80-100 (Ariana, Post Malone). 2019-2021: 80s synth pop revival 110-120 (The Weeknd "Blinding Lights" 171 = 85.5 half, Dua Lipa "Future Nostalgia"), bedroom pop 80-100 (Billie Eilish, Clairo). 2022-2025: Afrobeats/amapiano-inflected pop 100-115, disco pop 115-125, country-pop crossover, Charli XCX hyperpop edges. Top 40 has no single dominant tempo; 100-120 is the center.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 108 BPM, swing 0-0.1)
1. Mid-tempo pop: kick 0:118 6:95 10:110 | clap+snap 4:122 12:122 | hats 8ths 85/55, 16ths 14:70 15:60 | tambourine 2,6,10,14:60 chorus only | crash 0 bar 1 of the chorus.
2. Dance pop (120): kick 0,4,8,12:122 | clap 4,12:120 | open hat 2,6,10,14:100 | closed 16ths 75-45 | snare build 16ths last bar of the pre.
3. Trap-pop (90): kick 0:120 7:100 10:108 | clap 4:118 12:118 | hats 8ths 85/55 w/ 16th triplets at 12-15 | 808 follows roots | snaps 4, 12.
4. Verse (any): kick 0:105 10:95 | snap only 4,12:85 (no clap) | hats sparse or none | the chorus adds clap, hats, tambourine, crash, wider bass.

## Bass
- TYPE: synth bass (saw/square w/ lowpass, slight glide) or an 808 on roots w/ long notes and occasional octave pops; indie/bedroom = picked or fingered electric w/ chorus; dance pop = offbeat 8th synth bass sidechained; register E1-E2; locks to the kick on 1, holds through the chorus, moves on chord changes.
- Live (verified): Hip-Hop Sub Bass, Sub808Bass (trap-pop), Analog Saw Bass, June Oh Bass (synth pop), Fine Line Bass (indie), Deep Wave Bass (dance pop).

## Harmony & keys
- Diatonic 4-chord loops, often starting on vi for a bittersweet feel: vi-IV-I-V (Am-F-C-G), I-V-vi-IV, I-vi-IV-V (50s), IV-I-V-vi, vi-V-IV-V; borrowed iv (Fm in C) for the pre-chorus lift, a key change (+1 or +2 st) for the last chorus (rare now). Voicings: piano/pluck w/ the melody's top note on top, pads sustained, 7ths sparingly (add9/sus2 common). Keys C, G, D, A, F, Bb, Eb, minor relatives.
- Instruments: piano, plucks, supersaw/retro synth chords, guitar (clean/strummed), pads, strings on the bridge, vocal chop pads.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- Topline w/ a short repeated motif (melodic math: repetition, rhythm change on repeat), a 2-bar synth/pluck riff that answers the vocal and carries the post-chorus "drop", vocal chop stabs, a pre-chorus climb (ascending melody, hats 16ths, riser), a stop or dropout on the last beat before the chorus, the chorus one layer bigger each time.

## Textures & FX
- Risers and white-noise swells into the chorus, reverse snare/cymbal, filtered/lo-fi intro that opens, finger snaps, tambourine, vocal chops (pitched ±3 st), stutter edits, sub drop into the chorus, room/plate on claps.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4-8 (riff or filtered chorus) → verse 8-16 → pre-chorus 8 (lift) → chorus 8-16 → post-chorus 8 (riff/drop) → verse 8 → pre 8 → chorus → bridge 8 (new chord, stripped) → final chorus 16 (biggest). Total 2:30-3:20. Fills every 4-8 bars, dropout before choruses.

## Mix signature
- Vocal on top, drums punchy and bright, kick/808 share the low end (sidechain or EQ split), chorus wider and louder than the verse, master loud and clean.

## References (verify)
- Dua Lipa "Don't Start Now" / "Levitating", The Weeknd "Blinding Lights", Ariana Grande "7 rings" / "no tears left to cry", Billie Eilish "bad guy", Harry Styles "As It Was", Olivia Rodrigo "good 4 u", Taylor Swift "Anti-Hero", Charli XCX "360", Sabrina Carpenter "Espresso".

## Common mistakes
- Kick and 808 both owning the sub (mud), a dull single-layer snare, no pre-chorus lift, verses as busy as choruses, no post-chorus hook, a 5-minute form, random key changes, triads w/ no top-note movement.
