# Ambient (drone, generative, cinematic textures)
slug: ambient
aliases: ambient, ambient music, drone, drone music, ambient pad, soundscape, atmospheric, cinematic ambient, dark ambient, ambient techno (adjacent), chillout, downtempo ambient, meditation music, brian eno style, generative ambient
tempo: 60-90 nominal (often free time); ambient techno 115-125

## Summary
Ambient, 60-80 BPM nominal (often no grid). Drums: usually none; when present a soft felt kick heartbeat on 1 (or every 2 bars), brushed snare ghosts, shaker/rain textures, sparse rim clicks, reversed hits, all in long reverb. Kick: "soft felt kick heartbeat, deep and muffled, 500 ms, far away, reverb". Snare: "brushed snare ghost note, soft, long hall, 1 s". Pattern (if any): kick 0:70 (bar 1 only), rim 11:40, shaker swell steps 8-15 rising 30-60. Bass: a drone sub or slow pad bass (sine/filtered saw, 2-4 s attack) on the root or a pedal tone, C1-C2, changes every 4-8 bars. Chords: modal and slow: Lydian/Dorian/Aeolian pads, sus2/sus4/add9/maj7 stacks, one chord per 4-8 bars, pedal tones, stacked 5ths, one voice moving at a time; keys D, A, E, C, F. Hook: a 3-5 note motif on bells/piano/plucked guitar w/ long delay, repeating irregularly (generative loops of unequal lengths). Tex: granular stretches, field recordings, tape hiss, shimmer reverb, dotted delays, reversed piano, noise swells, bowed metals. Form: 3-4 layers fading in over 2-4 min, a peak, a slow fade; or generative 7/11/13-bar loops. Avoid: an audible grid, short reverbs, fast chord changes, busy leads.

## Era & tempo
- Brian Eno "Discreet Music" 1975 / "Ambient 1: Music for Airports" 1978: tape loops of different lengths (generative), "as ignorable as it is interesting". 90s: Aphex Twin "Selected Ambient Works Vol. II", The Orb, Global Communication, Biosphere (ambient techno/IDM 90-125). 2000s-2010s: Stars of the Lid, Tim Hecker, William Basinski (tape decay), Hammock, Nils Frahm/Ólafur Arnalds (modern classical crossover), Boards of Canada (adjacent). 2020s: lo-fi ambient playlists, Hania Rani, Kelly Lee Owens (ambient techno). Tempo is nominal; most pieces have no audible pulse.

- Hats/texture: "TrackType: SFX, soft shaker swell like rain, 2 seconds, filtered"; "TrackType: SFX, reversed cymbal swell, 1.5 seconds, washed"
- Perc: rim click (sparse), bowed cymbal, singing bowl, wood knock in a large room, granular clicks, vinyl crackle, breath.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 72 BPM nominal)
1. Heartbeat: kick 0:70 every 2 bars | rim 11:40 bar 2 | shaker swell 8-15 rising 30→60 | reversed cymbal 14-15 into bar 1 | everything else free.
2. Ambient techno (120): kick 0,4,8,12:95 (soft, sidechains the pad) | closed hats 2,6,10,14:50 washed | clap 12:60 w/ 2 s reverb | no fills.
3. Generative loops: layer A 7 bars (pad), layer B 11 bars (bell motif), layer C 13 bars (field recording swell): never align, no drums.
4. Cinematic pulse: sub pulse 8ths at 40-50 velocity w/ sidechain on the pad (Hans Zimmer style), a riser every 16 bars, one impact at the peak.

## Bass
- TYPE: a drone/pedal sub (sine or filtered saw, 2-4 s attack, slow LFO on the filter) on the root or a pedal tone (the 5th), changing every 4-8 bars or never; sometimes a bowed/cello-like low pad; register C1-C2; no rhythm, follows the pad root. Ambient techno: a soft dub-techno chord bass or a sidechained sub.
- Live (verified): Boom Sub Bass (long notes), Deep In Dark, Pure Minor, Wash Bass; pads as bass: Polar Pad one octave down.

## Harmony & keys
- Modal: Lydian (bright, #4), Dorian, Aeolian; sus2/sus4/add9/maj7/maj9 stacks, stacked 5ths, pedal tones with chords moving above (I-IV over a I pedal), one chord per 4-8 bars, voice leading where one note moves at a time, open voicings across 3 octaves. Keys D, A, E, C, F, Bb major / A, E, D minor.
- Instruments: analog/wavetable pads w/ slow attack, granular stretched piano/strings/guitar, felt piano, bowed guitar (E-bow), choir/vocal pads, bells/celesta, modular drones, field recordings.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- A 3-5 note motif (bells, felt piano, plucked guitar) repeated at irregular intervals through a long delay (dotted 8th + 1/2 note) and shimmer reverb; a swell that peaks every 16-32 bars; a melody fragment that only appears twice; the tape loop that slowly degrades (Basinski).

## Textures & FX
- Granular time-stretch (grain delay, freeze), shimmer reverb (octave up), convolution halls (8-20 s), long delays w/ filter feedback, tape wow/flutter and hiss, reversed piano/guitar/cymbals, field recordings (rain, wind, sea, room tone, distant traffic, birds), filtered white/pink noise swells, bit-reduction on a pad layer, sidechain from a silent "ghost" pulse for breathing.

## Arrangement
- No sections: 3-4 layers fade in over 1-3 minutes each (drone → pad → motif → texture), overlap at a peak (all layers, one impact or swell), then subtract one layer at a time; total 5-12 minutes. Generative: loops of coprime lengths (7, 11, 13 bars) running against each other. Ambient techno: 32-bar phrases w/ very slow filter openings.

## Mix signature
- Wide and deep: pads wide, drone mono-low, reverbs long and dark (highpassed), dynamics preserved (no limiter slam), gentle highs, everything slightly blurred.

## References (verify)
- Brian Eno "Music for Airports 1/1" / "An Ending (Ascent)", Aphex Twin "Rhubarb" (#3), Stars of the Lid "Requiem for Dying Mothers", William Basinski "dlp 1.1", Tim Hecker "Harmony in Ultraviolet", Boards of Canada "Dayvan Cowboy" (adjacent), Hammock "Mono No Aware", Nils Frahm "Says", Biosphere "Poa Alpina".

## Common mistakes
- An audible grid or steady kick (unless ambient techno), short/bright reverbs, chord changes every bar, busy bright leads, flattening the dynamics w/ compression, no field recording or noise layer (it sounds synthetic), pads w/ fast attacks, forgetting a slow LFO on the filter so nothing evolves.
