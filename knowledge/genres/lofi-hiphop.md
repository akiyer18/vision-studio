# Lo-fi Hip Hop
slug: lofi-hiphop
aliases: lofi, lo-fi, lo fi, lofi hip hop, lo-fi hip hop, lofi beats, chillhop, study beats, nujabes, jazzhop, chill hop, lofi beat
tempo: 70-90

## Summary
Lo-fi hip hop, 72-88 BPM, swing 0.25-0.4, drums soft and dusty with 2-3 elements: muffled round kick, soft papery snare (or rim), lightly swung hats, occasional shaker. Kick: "TrackType: SFX, soft muffled lo-fi kick, round thump, no click, 300 ms, tape-saturated, lowpassed". Snare: "soft dusty lo-fi snare, papery, 200 ms, vinyl texture, dry". Pattern: kick 0:105 7:85 10:98 | snare 4:100 12:100 (dragging 20 ms) | hats 8ths 85/50, missing one every bar | shaker 16ths at 30. Bass: TYPE = warm sine/low-passed electric or upright, 1-2 notes per bar on roots, lands with the kick, slightly behind. Harmony: jazz: maj7, m7, m9, 11ths, ii-V-I in minor, borrowed chords; Rhodes/Wurli, piano, nylon guitar, vibes, pitch-wobbled. Textures: vinyl crackle, tape hiss, rain, pitch wobble, lowpass everything, vocal snippets, foley. Form: 2-4 bar loop with filter/texture changes every 8 bars, 2-3 minutes. Mistakes: clean bright drums, 808 sub, too many layers, no swing, static loop with no texture changes, trap hats.

## Era & tempo
- 2015-now (Chillhop/ChilledCow lineage, roots in Nujabes, Dilla, Madlib). 70-90 BPM, heavy swing, drums 3 elements max, melodic content from jazz chords or chopped jazz records. Everything is softened: lowpass, tape, vinyl.

## Drum sounds (Stable Audio-ready)
- Kick: "TrackType: SFX, soft muffled lo-fi hip hop kick, round low thump, no click, 300 ms decay, tape saturation, lowpassed, dry"
- Snare: "TrackType: SFX, soft dusty lo-fi snare, papery mid crack, 200 ms, vinyl crackle texture, dry"; alt rimshot: "woody rim click, lo-fi"
- Hats: "TrackType: SFX, soft closed hi-hat, dull, lowpassed, 60 ms, swung feel"; open "soft open hat, dark, 250 ms"
- Perc: shaker 16ths very quiet, finger snap (soft), tambourine muffled, foley clicks (pen, cup).

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, swing 0.3, 80 BPM)
1. Classic head-nod: kick 0:105 7:85 10:98 | snare 4:100 12:100 (play 15-25 ms late) | hats 8ths 85/50 with step 10 missing | shaker 16ths 30.
2. Dilla-ish wobble: kick 0:110 6:88 9:80 10:100 | snare 4:98 12:102 late | hats 8ths uneven 90/45/80/55 | rim 14:50.
3. Minimal: kick 0:100 8:95 | snare 4:95 12:95 | no hats, vinyl crackle carries the 16ths.
4. Fill (every 8 bars): snare drops out bar 8, a reverse snare or a vinyl stop on 15.

## Bass
- TYPE: warm sine sub or lowpassed electric/upright sample; soft attack. Register E1-A2. Pattern: 1-2 notes per bar on roots (plus the 5th), lands with or slightly behind the kick (10-20 ms), holds; occasional walk-up on bar 4.
- Live (verified): Mello Bass, Piano Bass, Fine Line Bass with Auto Filter lowpass ~250 Hz.

## Harmony & keys
- Jazz harmony: Cmaj7-Am7-Dm7-G7 (I-vi-ii-V), ii-V-i in minor (Dm7b5-G7-Cm9), Fmaj7-Em7-Am7, borrowed iv; 7ths, 9ths, 11ths, sus chords, soft voice leading, often played as 2 bars per chord.
- Instruments: Rhodes/Wurlitzer (chorus, slight detune, pitch wobble), upright piano (felt), nylon guitar, vibraphone, muted trumpet/sax snippets, flute. Chopped jazz/soul record alternative.
- Movement: pitch wobble (0.2-0.4 Hz LFO on pitch ±10 cents), lowpass filter sweeps, tape stop.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- A short melodic phrase every 4 bars (piano/flute), a vocal snippet (movie dialogue) as a hook, the chord loop filtered down for 8 bars and reopened; rain/ambience intro.

## Textures & FX
- Vinyl crackle bed (constant), tape hiss, rain/room tone, pitch wobble, lowpass on the master, tape stop/start, vocal snippets, foley (pages, cups), reverse piano swells.

## Arrangement
- Loop-based: intro 4 (texture + chords) → full loop 16 → variation 16 (drop kick for 4 bars, add shaker) → breakdown 8 (filter down, drums out) → full 16 → outro (tape stop). Changes every 8 bars via texture/filter, not new parts.

## Mix signature
- Soft, lowpassed (master lowpass ~12 kHz), drums behind the chords, bass warm, everything slightly saturated, narrow stereo.

## References (verify)
- Nujabes "Feather"/"Luv(sic)", J Dilla "Donuts" (Workinonit), Jinsang "Affection", Idealism "Controlla", Tomppabeats, Knxwledge, Chillhop Essentials compilations.

## Common mistakes
- Bright clean drums, 808 sub, four or more drum layers, no swing, chords too plain (triads), no texture bed, no changes for 2 minutes, trap hat rolls, loud hats.
