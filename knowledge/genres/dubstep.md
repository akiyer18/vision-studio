# Dubstep / UK Bass (140)
slug: dubstep
aliases: dubstep, uk bass, 140, uk dubstep, deep dubstep, brostep, riddim, wobble bass, skrillex style, halfstep, half step, grime (adjacent), bass music
tempo: 138-142 (half-time, snare on 3)

## Summary
Dubstep, 140 BPM half-time: kick on 1 (and sync'd spots), snare on beat 3 (step 8) only, sparse hats/ride, tons of space, sub bass as the lead. Kick: "deep dubstep kick, round sub thump w/ soft click, 300 ms, dry". Snare: "big dubstep snare, cracking layered snare w/ long dark reverb tail, 400 ms, compressed". Pattern (UK deep): kick 0:122 10:105 (bar2 0:122 5:90 11:100) | snare 8:127 | hats 8ths 70/45 sparse or ride 2,6,10,14 | shaker 16ths 35 | perc click 3,13:55 | rim 14:60. Bass: sub sine (pure, long, the actual bass) PLUS a mid bass: wobble (LFO on a lowpass, 1/4-1/8-1/16 sync changing per bar), reese, or growl (FM/wavetable); bass plays the drop's rhythm: 0 (long), 6, 8 (with snare) or 10, w/ the wobble rate as the hook. Chords: dark minor (Fm, Gm, Em), single chord or i-VI, atmospheric pads, eerie leads, melodic dubstep uses piano/supersaw chords i-VI-III-VII. Tex: risers, reverse snare, vocal chops, sub drops, "wub", sirens (grime), vinyl/rain in intros. Form: 32 intro, 16 build, 32 drop (vary every 8), 16 breakdown, drop 2. Avoid: snare on 2 and 4 (becomes 140 techno), too many hats, sub and mid bass not split.

## Era & tempo
- 2003-08 South London (Skream, Benga, Digital Mystikz, Burial adjacent): 138-142, half-time, sub-led, dark. 2010-13 US brostep (Skrillex, Excision): aggressive mid-bass, dense drops. Riddim (2015+): repetitive wobble patterns. Deep dubstep/UK bass today: sparse, sub, dub influences.

## Drum sounds (Stable Audio-ready)
- Kick: "TrackType: SFX, deep dubstep kick drum, round sub thump with a soft click, 300 ms decay, dry one-shot" (brostep: "punchy clicky kick, 150 ms")
- Snare: "TrackType: SFX, big dubstep snare, cracking layered snare with a long dark reverb tail, 400 ms, compressed"; alt "rimshot-heavy snare, metallic, 200 ms"
- Hats: closed "TrackType: SFX, soft closed hi-hat, 50 ms, dark"; ride "ride cymbal ping, 500 ms"
- Perc: shaker 16ths (quiet), rim clicks, woodblock, reverse snare, tom hits, metallic clanks.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 140 BPM, half-time, straight)
1. UK deep (DMZ): kick 0:122 10:105 | bar2 kick 0:122 5:90 11:100 | snare 8:127 | hats 8ths 70/45 sparse (skip 4 and 12) | ride 2,6,10,14:60 on drops | shaker 16ths 35 | perc click 3:55 13:55 | rim 14:60.
2. Brostep (Skrillex): kick 0:127 6:110 (bar2 0,9) | snare 8:127 (+clap) | hats 16ths 90-60 with 32nd stutters | crash 0 on drop | bass hits exactly with kick and snare.
3. Riddim: kick 0:127 | snare 8:127 | minimal hats 8ths 60 | bass wobble pattern 0,3,6,8,11,14 (the rhythm is the bass).
4. Build (8-16 bars): snare roll accelerating 8ths→16ths→32nds, riser, kick out, drop silence 1 beat.

## Bass
- TYPE: split design: (1) pure sine sub, mono, long notes (the real bass, 40-60 Hz); (2) mid bass: wobble (lowpass with tempo-synced LFO: 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, triplets, rate changes per bar), reese (detuned saws), growl/talking bass (FM/wavetable + formant filter), riddim "wub" (square + LFO). Register sub F1-C2, mids F2-F3.
- Pattern: the drop rhythm: 0 (hold 2-3 beats), 6:short, 8 with the snare, 10-11 pickup into bar 2; wobble LFO rate = the hook (e.g. 1/4 bar 1, 1/8 bar 2, 1/16 bar 3, triplet bar 4). 1-4 notes per bar; sub follows the mid bass roots.
- Live (verified): sub = Boom Sub Bass or Simpler sine one-shot (long); mids = Wobble Bass, Wobble 2 Bass, Bit Growl Bass, Reso Blaster; Auto Filter lowpass with LFO sync 1/8 on the mid layer; Saturator on mids only.

## Harmony & keys
- Dark minor (Fm, Gm, Em, C#m): one chord or i-VI (Fm-Db), i-VII; melodic dubstep: i-VI-III-VII with supersaws/piano and an uplifting lead.
- Instruments: eerie pads, sine/FM leads, strings (dark), piano (melodic), vocal chops pitched, organ (dub), bells.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The wobble rhythm; a vocal phrase before the drop ("drop silence" then bass); a melodic lead in the intro/breakdown returning over the drop; reese slide into bar 1; call-and-response between two bass sounds (A on bars 1-2, B on 3-4).

## Textures & FX
- Long risers (16 bars), reverse snare/cymbal, sub drops, impacts, vocal chops, rain/vinyl in intros, siren (grime), tape stops, delay throws on snares, "wub" FX stabs.

## Arrangement
- Intro 32 (atmos, filtered drums) → build 16 (snare roll + riser) → drop 32 (bass pattern, variation every 8: LFO rate/sound swap) → breakdown 16-32 (pads, vocal) → build 8-16 → drop 2 (32, new bass sounds) → outro 16. Drop silence (1 beat) before each drop.

## Mix signature
- Sub mono and loud, mid bass saturated and wide, snare huge with reverb, kick short, hats quiet, lots of space.

## References (verify)
- Skream "Midnight Request Line", Digital Mystikz "Anti-War Dub", Benga & Coki "Night", Burial "Archangel" (adjacent), Skrillex "Scary Monsters and Nice Sprites", Excision, Rusko "Cockney Thug", Virtual Riot (riddim/melodic).

## Common mistakes
- Snare on 2 and 4 at 140; busy hats; one bass sound carrying sub and mids (muddy); no space in the drop; wobble at one rate all track; no drop silence/riser; melody over the drop at the same volume as the bass.
