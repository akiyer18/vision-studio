# Deep House
slug: deep-house
aliases: deep house, deephouse, classic house, chicago house, soulful house, kerri chandler, larry heard, house music, deep
tempo: 118-125

## Summary
Deep house, 120-124 BPM, swing 0.1-0.2 on hats. Drums: warm round 909/707-ish kick (not clicky), soft clap or snare on 2 and 4, open hat on every offbeat (the "and"), closed hats 16ths with ghosts, shaker, rim/clave. Kick: "TrackType: SFX, warm round deep house kick, soft rounded attack, 70 Hz body, 350 ms, slightly compressed, dry". Clap: "soft 909-style clap with a short warm room, 200 ms". Pattern: kick 0,4,8,12:118 | clap 4,12:105 | open hat 2,6,10,14:95 | closed 16ths 70-45-60-40 | shaker 16ths 35 | rim 3,11:60. Bass: TYPE = deep round synth sub (sine/triangle, or filtered saw) playing offbeats and syncopated 8ths BETWEEN kicks, 1-note or root-5th-b7, with occasional octave; sidechained feel. Harmony: minor 7th/9th chords, 2-4 chord loops (i7-iv7-VII-VI, ii-V), Rhodes/organ stabs on the offbeat, warm pads, vocal samples; keys Fm, Gm, Am. Textures: vinyl, vocal chops/spoken word, filtered pad swells, reverse crash, white noise sweeps (subtle). Form: 32-bar intro (DJ), 16-bar builds, breakdown with pads, 8-bar re-intro of the kick. Mistakes: hard EDM kick, no offbeat open hat, bass on every kick, triads, no swing, drop-oriented arrangement.

## Era & tempo
- Chicago 1986-90 roots (Larry Heard "Can You Feel It", Marshall Jefferson), 90s NY deep (Kerri Chandler, Masters At Work), 2010s revival. 118-125 BPM, 909/707/808 machine drums, jazzy chords, soulful vocal samples, long DJ-friendly forms.

## Drum sounds (Stable Audio-ready)
- Kick: "TrackType: SFX, warm round deep house kick drum, soft rounded attack, 70 Hz body, 350 ms decay, slightly compressed, dry one-shot"
- Clap: "TrackType: SFX, soft 909-style clap with a short warm room, 200 ms, dry"; snare alt "soft snappy 707 snare, 180 ms"
- Hats: closed "TrackType: SFX, 909 closed hi-hat, soft metallic, 60 ms, dry"; open "909 open hi-hat, airy sizzle, 280 ms, slightly dark"
- Perc: shaker 16ths, rim/clave, conga (low), ride on the hook, tambourine (soulful).

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 122 BPM, swing 0.15 on hats/shaker)
1. Classic deep: kick 0:118 4:116 8:118 12:116 | clap 4:105 12:105 | open hat 2:95 6:95 10:95 14:95 | closed 16ths 70-45-60-40 | shaker 16ths 35 | rim 3:60 11:60.
2. Kerri-style (dusted): kick 4 on floor 118 | snare+clap 4,12 | open hat 2,6,10,14:90 | closed hat ghosts 1,5,9,13:45 | conga 7:70 15:60 | ride 8ths on the hook.
3. Minimal deep (Larry Heard): kick 4otf | clap 4,12 | open hat 2,6,10,14 | no closed hats | cowbell/clave 3,10 at 55.
4. Fill: open hat on 15 held, clap roll 14:70 15:90 every 8 bars; kick drops out bar 32.

## Bass
- TYPE: deep round synth sub (sine/triangle or lowpassed saw with slow filter), sometimes a sampled electric. Register F1-C2. Pattern: plays BETWEEN the kicks: offbeat 8ths (steps 2,6,10,14) or a syncopated root-5th-b7 figure (0:short, 3, 6, 10, 13), 1-2 notes per bar in deep/minimal; sidechain-like ducking to the kick.
- Live (verified): Deep Wave Bass, Deep In Dark, Boom Sub Bass; Auto Filter lowpass 400-800 Hz with slight LFO.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor 7th and 9th chords, 2-4 chord loops: i7-iv7 (Fm7-Bbm7), i7-VII-VI (Gm7-F-Eb), ii7-V7 vamps, maj7 for uplift. Keys Fm, Gm, Am, Cm.
- Voicings: Rhodes/organ stabs on the offbeat (step 2 or 6), sustained warm pad under, piano chords on the hook, filtered strings; vocal sample phrases pitched to key.
- Instruments: Rhodes, Hammond-like organ stabs, warm analog pads (Juno/Prophet-like), piano, sampled vocals, muted trumpet.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- A chord stab figure on the offbeat; a vocal phrase (one line, repeated, filtered in/out); a pad swell into each 16-bar phrase; bass riff variation on the hook; filter opening on the pad over 8 bars.

## Textures & FX
- Vinyl crackle, spoken word/vocal snippets, reverse crash into phrases, subtle white-noise sweeps, dub delay throws on the vocal/stab, lowpass filter automation on the pad.

## Arrangement
- Intro 32 (drums, bass, hat) → 16 add stabs → 16 add pad/vocal (hook) → breakdown 16 (kick out, pads, vocal) → re-intro kick 8 → 32 full → outro 32 (strip back). Changes every 8/16 bars; fills every 8.

## Mix signature
- Warm, round low end (kick and bass share 50-90 Hz by ducking), soft top, chords wide, vocal centered, subtle saturation.

## References (verify)
- Larry Heard/Mr. Fingers "Can You Feel It", Kerri Chandler "Rain", Masters At Work, Moodymann "Shades of Jae", Disclosure early EPs, Lane 8/Anjunadeep (modern melodic deep).

## Common mistakes
- Hard clicky EDM kick, no offbeat open hat (the house engine), bass on every kick, triad pads, no swing or shaker, trap-style drops, bright hats, 8-bar structures (house breathes in 16/32).
