# Future Bass
slug: future-bass
aliases: future bass, futurebass, kawaii bass, melodic bass, flume style, illenium style, chill trap, future trap, wonky chords, supersaw chords beat
tempo: 140-160 (felt as half-time 70-80), some at 128-135

## Summary
Future bass, 150 BPM half-time (feels 75), swing 0-0.1. Drums: half-time backbeat: snare/clap on beat 3 (step 8) layered w/ a snap and a big reverb, punchy short kick on 1 plus pickups, trap hats in 8ths w/ 16th and triplet rolls, crash on the drop, snare rolls and risers in the build, a sub drop. Kick: "future bass kick, punchy short click with a tuned 808-ish body, 300 ms, clean". Snare: "huge future bass snare layered with a clap and a snap, bright crack, long reverb tail, 600 ms". Pattern (drop): kick 0:127 10:105 | snare+clap 8:127 | hats 8ths 90/60 w/ 16ths at 12-15 | snap 8 | crash 0. Bass: tuned 808 sub (long roots, glides on the drop) under the chords, C1-G1, in the kick gaps. Chords: the genre IS the chord stack: detuned supersaws (7-16 voices) on 7th/9th/add9 chords in rhythmic stabs w/ sidechain pump and a pitch-bend "wow" into each hit, major keys (I-V-vi-IV, vi-IV-I-V, IV-V-iii-vi), pitched-up vocal chops as melody. Tex: formant-shifted vocal chops, bells/plucks, sub drop, noise risers, foley snaps, reverse cymbals. Form: intro 8-16, build 8-16, drop 16, break 16, build, drop 2. Avoid: four-on-the-floor kicks, static chords w/o bend/sidechain, hats w/o rolls.

## Era & tempo
- 2012-2015 origins: Flume (self-titled), Cashmere Cat, Wave Racer, Rustie, Hudson Mohawke (trap/maximalism), Mr. Carmack. 2016-2019 peak: Illenium, San Holo, Marshmello (pop future bass), Louis the Child, Flume "Skin", kawaii/future funk offshoots (Snail's House). 2020s: melodic bass (Illenium/Seven Lions) 150, pop crossovers 128-140. Tempo 140-160 half-time; some at 128-135 (Flume "Never Be Like You" 92 feel).

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 150 BPM half-time, swing 0-0.1)
1. Drop: kick 0:127 10:105 | snare+clap+snap 8:127 | hats 8ths 90/60, 16ths at 12-15 (85-55), triplet roll on step 14-15 bar 4 | crash 0 bar 1 | chord stabs 0, 3, 6, 8, 11, 14 (sidechained, pitch bend up).
2. Chill drop: kick 0:120 6:95 11:100 | snare 8:120 | hats 8ths 80/50 | snaps 8, 13:60 | vocal chop on 2, 10 | chords whole-ish w/ pump.
3. Break/verse: kick 0:100 | snare 8:95 (dry, small) | hats sparse 4, 12:60 | plucks carry the chords | sub long notes.
4. Build (8-16 bars): snare 8ths → 16ths → 32nds rising pitch, hats 16ths, riser, chord stabs every 2 beats then every beat, 1-beat silence + vocal chop before the drop.

## Bass
- TYPE: tuned 808 sub (sine w/ slight saturation), long notes on chord roots (glide between roots on the drop), one octave below the chords (C1-G1), sidechained lightly to the kick; sometimes a reese/growl layer for melodic bass (Illenium). Never busy: the chords carry the rhythm.
- Live (verified): Sub808Bass, Boom Sub Bass, Distorted Sine Bass (growl layer), Hip-Hop Sub Bass.

## Harmony & keys
- Major keys w/ emotional 4-chord loops: I-V-vi-IV (C-G-Am-F), vi-IV-I-V, IV-V-iii-vi, I-iii-IV-V; 7ths/9ths/add9/sus2 voicings on supersaw stacks (7-16 voices, 1-2 octave spread), pitch-bend "wow" (pitch envelope +2 to +12 st sweeping into each hit over 60-150 ms, or LFO on pitch), hard sidechain pump (attack 0, release 150-250 ms); plucks/bells double the top note; keys C, G, D, F, A, E major.
- Instruments: supersaw chord stack, FM bells, plucks (saw w/ filter env), chopped vocals (formant up), piano in the intro, sub.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The chord-stab rhythm itself (syncopated 0, 3, 6, 8, 11, 14), pitch-bent "wow" chords, chopped vocal syllable melody ("oh-oh") pitched +2 to +5 st w/ formant shift, a bell/pluck counter-melody on the drop top, a 1-beat silence + vocal chop before the drop, drop 2 = same chords w/ a new top melody and extra snare fills.

## Textures & FX
- Vocal chops (pitched, formant-shifted, reversed), white-noise risers and sweeps, impacts/booms, sub drop, foley clicks/snaps, reverse cymbal, shimmer reverb on bells, vinyl crackle only in chill variants, water/air ambiences.

## Arrangement
- Intro 8-16 (piano/plucks + chords + vocal chop) → verse/break 16 (sparse drums) → build 8-16 → drop 16 (chord stabs, half-time drums) → break 16 → build 8 → drop 2 16 (variation + extra fills) → outro 8. Fills every 4 bars (snare roll or hat triplet), silence before drops.

## Mix signature
- Chords huge and wide (mono below 150 Hz), snare enormous w/ reverb, kick short so the sub breathes, sub clean, hats crisp on top; heavy sidechain glue; bright and loud.

## References (verify)
- Flume "Holdin On" / "Never Be Like You", Cashmere Cat "Mirror Maru", Wave Racer "Streamers", Illenium "Fractures", San Holo "Light", Marshmello "Alone", Louis the Child "It's Strange", Snail's House "Pixel Galaxy" (kawaii).

## Common mistakes
- Four-on-the-floor kicks (it is half-time), static chords w/o pitch bend or pump, triads, hats w/o rolls, a busy bass fighting the chords, a muddy sub under full chord stacks (keep chords above 200 Hz), build w/o silence.
