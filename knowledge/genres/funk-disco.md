# Funk / Disco / Nu-Disco
slug: funk-disco
aliases: funk, disco, nu disco, nu-disco, nudisco, disco house, funky, p-funk, boogie, electro funk, french touch, daft punk style, nile rodgers style, chic style, funk beat, disco beat, groove
tempo: 100-120 (funk 95-115, disco 115-125, nu-disco 118-124)

## Summary
Funk/disco, 118-122 BPM (funk 100-110), swing 0.05-0.15 (hats 52-56%). Drums: live-feel acoustic kit: four-on-the-floor kick (disco) or a syncopated funk kick (1, "and" of 2, "a" of 3), fat compressed snare on 2 and 4 w/ a tight room, 16th hats played two-handed w/ velocity alternation 100/70 and an open hat on every offbeat 8th (disco "pea-soup"), congas/bongos, tambourine 8ths, cowbell, claps on the chorus. Kick: "70s disco kick, round punchy acoustic, felt beater, 350 ms, tight room, dry". Snare: "fat 70s funk snare, big compressed crack, short room, 250 ms". Pattern (disco): kick 0,4,8,12:118 | snare 4,12:120 | open hat 2,6,10,14:105 | closed 16ths 100-70-85-65 | tambourine 8ths 60 | conga 3:70 11:70 bar 2. Bass: electric fingered or slap bass, octave jumps (root + octave on 8ths), chromatic walk-ups, ghost notes, locked to the kick on 1 and busy on the "and"s; nu-disco = synth bass (Moog-ish) in octaves, sidechained; register E1-G2. Chords: 7ths/9ths/m7/dom7 vamps (Em7-A7, Fm7-Bb7), ii-V loops, dominant 9ths, string stabs, Rhodes/clav comping, Nile Rodgers 16th funk guitar (muted chucks). Hook: guitar riff + horn stabs, string line, a vocal chant; nu-disco: filtered loop opening up, talkbox/vocoder. Tex: strings, horns, congas, hand claps, vocoder, filter sweeps, tape, vinyl. Form: intro 8, verse 16, chorus 16, breakdown 16 (drums + bass + filtered guitar), chorus. Avoid: stiff quantized hats, 808s, no bassline movement, pads instead of stabs/strings, missing offbeat open hats.

## Era & tempo
- Funk 1967-79 (James Brown, Sly, P-Funk, Earth Wind & Fire): 95-115, live bands, syncopated kicks, slap bass, horn sections. Disco 1974-80 (Chic, Giorgio Moroder, Bee Gees, Donna Summer): 115-125, four-on-the-floor, open hat offbeats, strings, orchestral hits. Boogie/electro funk 1980-85 (Zapp, Prince, Kashif): 105-115, drum machines + synth bass + talkbox. French touch 1995-2005 (Daft Punk, Cassius, Alan Braxe): 118-128, filtered disco loops, sidechain. Nu-disco 2008-now (Breakbot, Todd Terje, Purple Disco Machine, Dua Lipa "Future Nostalgia"): 118-124, live-feel + synth bass + modern mix. Keys often minor 7 vamps (Em, Am, Dm) or major 7 (Fmaj7, Ebmaj7).

1. Disco: kick 0,4,8,12:118 | snare 4,12:120 | open hat 2,6,10,14:105 | closed 16ths 100-70-85-65 | tambourine 8ths 60 | conga open 3:70 11:70, slap 7:60.
2. Funk (105): kick 0:120 6:100 10:112 (bar 2: 0, 3, 10) | snare 4:122 12:122 | ghost snare 7:40 15:45 | closed hats 16ths 95-65-80-60 | open hat 14:90 | cowbell 0, 8:70.
3. Nu-disco: kick 4otf 120 | clap+snare 4,12:118 | open hat 2,6,10,14:100 | closed 16ths 90-60 | shaker 16ths 55 | rim 11:65 | filtered guitar chucks 16ths.

## Bass
- TYPE: fingered or slap electric bass w/ octave jumps (root on the beat, octave on the "and"), chromatic walk-ups into chord changes, ghost notes (muted 16ths), pops on the "a"s; nu-disco/boogie = synth bass (Moog/Juno) in octaves on 8ths w/ a filter envelope, sidechained to the kick; register E1-G2; locks to the kick on 1 and fills the "and"s.
- Live (verified): Electric Bass Drive, Rock Electric Bass (picked), Smack Wah Bass (funk wah), Funk Saw Bass, Dank Moug / Muugy (Moog boogie), Rump Shaker Bass (nu-disco).

## Harmony & keys
- Dominant 7th/9th vamps (E9 for 8 bars, James Brown style), m7 two-chord loops (Em7-A7, Dm7-G7), ii-V-I in major w/ 9ths/13ths (Chic: Am7-D7-Gmaj7), m9 to IV7, modal mixture (bVII). Voicings: Rhodes/clav rootless 7th/9th stabs on the offbeats, string lines in octaves, horn section stabs (root-5th-9th), Nile Rodgers 16th-note muted guitar chucks on the 2 and 4 chord tones.
- Instruments: clavinet, Rhodes, string section, horn section (trumpets/sax), funk guitar (clean Strat, muted 16ths), Moog bass, talkbox/vocoder (boogie), piano octaves (disco).

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The guitar riff (2 bars, 16th chucks + a melodic lick), horn stabs answering the vocal, a string line that climbs over 4 bars, a chanted vocal hook, the filtered loop opening (nu-disco/French touch), the breakdown's bass + congas, a snare roll + string swell into the chorus, a key lift on the last chorus (disco).

## Textures & FX
- Strings (swells and staccato), horn stabs, congas/bongos, hand claps, tambourine, cowbell, vocoder/talkbox, filter sweeps on the loop (lowpass closing/opening over 8-16 bars), tape saturation, vinyl crackle (nu-disco), phaser on Rhodes, chorus on bass.

## Arrangement
- Intro 8 (drums + bass or filtered loop) → verse 16 → chorus 16 (horns/strings, claps) → verse 16 → chorus 16 → breakdown 16 (kick, hats, bass, congas, filtered guitar) → build 8 (strings swell, snare roll) → chorus 16-32 (full, key lift optional) → outro 16 (loop filters out). Fills every 8 bars (snare + tom or conga flurry).

## Mix signature
- Bass and kick tight and forward, snare fat and compressed, hats bright and panned (two-handed feel), strings wide, guitar mid-forward, light bus compression that pumps slightly (nu-disco more).

## Common mistakes
- Quantized stiff hats (play the two-handed 16ths w/ alternating velocity), no offbeat open hat, a static bass w/ no octaves or ghosts, pads instead of stabs/strings/horns, 808s, missing congas/tambourine, breakdowns that drop the bass (disco keeps bass and drums).
