# Industrial (EBM, industrial rock, industrial techno)
slug: industrial
aliases: industrial, industrial electronic, industrial techno, ebm, electronic body music, industrial rock, industrial metal, industrial hip hop, aggrotech, dark electro, power noise, rhythmic noise, nine inch nails, gesaffelstein, distorted kick techno, front 242, nitzer ebb, ministry, kmfdm, skinny puppy, throbbing gristle, cabaret voltaire, perc, ancient methods, boy harsher, 3teeth
tempo: 90-100 (industrial rock / industrial hip hop, half-time), 120-135 (EBM), 130-145 (industrial techno)

## Summary
Industrial, 90-100 BPM (industrial rock/hip hop, half-time backbeat), 120-135 (EBM), 130-145 (industrial techno), straight, swing 0. Kick: distorted/clipped, long rumble, mono ("industrial kick, clipped 909-style punch into a long distorted rumble, 55 Hz, 700 ms, overdriven, dry"). Snare: metallic, layered w/ sheet-metal/anvil/pipe hits, gated room, 250-400 ms. Hats/perc: machine noise, chains, hammers, air compressor, steam, found objects, bitcrushed. Pattern (138): kick 0,4,8,12:127 | metal hit 4,12:115 | hats 16ths 95-60 | chain 3,11:70. Bass: distorted saw/square: EBM 16th sequencer bass w/ octave jumps and accents, or a sub glued to the kick, filling the kick's gaps. Harmony: minor/phrygian, single-note drones, tritone stabs; keys Em, Dm, Am, Cm. Hooks: shouted vocals, sampled speech, feedback, siren leads. Tex: factory ambience, metal resonances, noise sweeps, tape/bitcrush, reverse hits. Form: 16-32-bar blocks, hard dropouts, breakdown = machine ambience, re-entry on a reversed hit. Mix: loud, dry, mid-forward, mono low end, distortion in stages. Avoid: clean kick, too much reverb, sounding like plain techno (needs metal, dropouts, distortion), no dynamics.

## Era & tempo
- Roots 1975-82 (Throbbing Gristle, Cabaret Voltaire, Einsturzende Neubauten): tape loops, found metal, no fixed tempo, speech samples; use as texture source, not as a groove template.
- EBM 1984-92 (Front 242, Nitzer Ebb, DAF, Skinny Puppy's harder side): 120-135, straight 4/4, sequenced 16th-note bass, shouted vocals, drum machines (DMX, Linn, 909) with industrial samples.
- Industrial rock/metal 1988-2000 (Ministry, NIN, KMFDM, Skinny Puppy, Marilyn Manson): 90-100 half-time grooves or 120-150 double-kick marches, drum machine + live drums, sampled guitars, Trent Reznor's hip-hop-tempo verses ("Closer" ~95).
- Industrial techno 1996-now (Surgeon, Regis, Ancient Methods, Perc, Blawan, SNTS, Rebekah, Paula Temple): 130-145, distorted four-on-the-floor, metal hits instead of claps, rumble.
- Modern industrial-leaning electronic/pop 2010-now (Gesaffelstein, HEALTH, 3TEETH, Boy Harsher, Death Grips, Sega Bodega-style distorted kicks): 90-130, hip-hop or synthpop forms with clipped kicks, EBM bass and metallic hits.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps per bar; step:velocity; all straight, swing 0)
1. Industrial rock / hip hop chorus (94 BPM, half-time feel, NIN "Closer" / Death Grips territory):
   kick 0:120 7:100 10:118 (bar 2: 0:120 6:95 10:118 14:85 distorted 808 under) | snare 4:127 12:127 (sheet-metal layer on both) | hats 8ths 100/70 crunchy, 16ths on 14,15 at 90 every other bar | chain hit 3:80 11:80 | pipe hit 15:70 bar 4 | reverse hit into 0 of the chorus.
2. Industrial rock march (104 BPM, Ministry / KMFDM): kick 0,2,4,6,8,10,12,14:115 (8th-note double kick) | snare 4,12:127 + anvil layer | hats 16ths 90-60 | crash 0 every 8 bars | hammer 8:100 every 2 bars.
3. EBM (128 BPM, Front 242 / Nitzer Ebb): kick 0,4,8,12:120 | snare 4,12:118 (metallic clang) | hats 8ths 95/65, open hat 14:90 | metal perc 2,10:80 | tom 7,15:85 (bar 4) | the 16th-note bass sequencer does the drive, keep the drums sparse.
4. Industrial techno (138 BPM, Perc / Ancient Methods): kick 0,4,8,12:127 (distorted, rumble tail) | NO clap; metal hit 4,12:115 (pipe/anvil) | closed hats 16ths 95-60-80-60 bitcrushed | chain 3,11:70 | air-compressor burst 14-15:90 every 4 bars | hammer 6:100 every 2 bars | rumble constant.
5. Power noise / rhythmic noise (140 BPM): kick 0,4,8,12:127 distorted beyond recognition | distorted noise snare 4,12:120 | noise 16ths 100-70 instead of hats | everything through one distortion stage, no low end below 50 Hz.
6. Modern pop-industrial (Gesaffelstein / HEALTH / Boy Harsher, 110-124): kick 0,4,8,12:127 clipped | snare 4,12:120 gated | hats 8ths 85/55 | sparse metallic perc 6:70 | dropout: all drums out on bar 8 beats 3-4, kick returns alone.
7. Breakdown: kick and bass out, factory ambience + drone + metal resonances for 8-16 bars; re-entry with a reversed metal hit and the kick alone for 4 bars before the snare returns.

## Bass
- TYPE: (a) EBM sequencer bass: distorted saw or square (Pro-One/SH-101/ARP-style), 16th notes on one root with octave jumps and accents (e.g. E1 E1 E2 E1 | E1 E2 E1 E1), short decay, filter opening with velocity; (b) industrial rock: distorted saw/square synth bass or picked bass through a fuzz, playing 8ths or riff-locked with the guitar; (c) industrial techno: the kick's rumble is the bass, plus an offbeat one-note square bass (steps 2,6,10,14) or a drone on the root; (d) modern: sine/808 sub glued to the clipped kick (sidechained hard) with a distorted mid layer on top.
- Movement vs kick: in EBM the bass runs constant 16ths and the kick sits inside it; in industrial rock/hip hop the bass hits WITH the kick on 1 and fills the gaps (and of 2, step 7, step 10) with the kick's pattern, sliding into the root on the chorus; in industrial techno the bass is the kick's tail, with the offbeat bass only in the gaps between kicks.
- Register: E1-E2 root, octave up for movement. Distortion staging: clean sub (sine) + distorted mid layer (saw/square through overdrive then a lowpass at 2 kHz) kept mono.
- Live (verified): Tough Electro Bass, Boom Sub Bass, Reso Blaster (sequencer bass with Operator/Analog saw into Saturator and Overdrive); rumble = Simpler with the kick tail + Reverb 100% wet lowpassed at 150 Hz.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor and phrygian (b2 is the industrial interval: E-F, A-Bb), single-note drones, tritone stabs (E-Bb), chromatic half-step riffs. Keys: Em, Dm, Am, Cm, F#m. Chords are rare: a power chord or a minor stab held for 8 bars, i-bII (Em-F), i-bVI-bVII (Em-C-D) in industrial rock.
- Instruments: detuned saw stabs, square-wave leads, FM metal tones, distorted organ/Rhodes (NIN), sampled strings pitched down, sampled guitar power chords (chugs, feedback), drone pads from resampled metal hits.
- EBM melody: 2-4 note sequence over the bass, played on a square or PWM lead, always short notes.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- Shouted/chanted vocal phrase (two-syllable commands, "Join in the chant"), sampled speech from films/news/documentaries chopped to the grid, guitar feedback swells into the chorus, siren-like rising leads (saw with pitch bend up a tritone), a one-note stab on the "and" of 2 every other bar, a drone that detunes over 8 bars, a metal hit as a melodic motif (pitched pipe hits playing a 3-note riff).
- Dropouts are the hook: everything stops for 2 beats and the kick comes back alone.

## Textures & FX
- Factory ambience beds (conveyor hum, hydraulics, distant clanks), long metal resonances (pipe/anvil through a convolution of a stairwell), white/pink noise sweeps with distortion before the filter, tape saturation and wow, bitcrush/sample-rate reduction on hats and perc, reverse metal hits into downbeats, feedback squeals, radio static, pitched-down vocal snippets, hard-gated reverb on snares (no tail), sub drops on dropouts.

## Arrangement
- Industrial rock/hip hop: intro 8 (ambience + drone, a metal hit) → verse 16 (kick/bass only, snare enters bar 9) → chorus 8 (full drums, guitars/stabs, shout) → verse 16 → chorus 8 → breakdown 8 (ambience, speech sample) → chorus 16 → outro 8 (machine ambience fades, one last metal hit).
- EBM: 8-bar intro (bass sequencer alone) → add kick 8 → add snare + vocal 16 → break 8 (bass out, drums + sample) → full 32 → outro.
- Industrial techno: 32-bar blocks like techno: intro 32 (kick + rumble + hats) → 32 add metal hits → 32 add noise/drone → breakdown 16-32 (kick out, factory ambience, hammer) → peak 64 → outro 32. Fills: hammer/air-compressor flurries on bar 8/16, never snare rolls.
- Everywhere: hard dropouts (full silence or kick alone) every 16 bars; dynamics come from removing elements, not from filters.

## Mix signature
- Loud, dry, mid-forward, abrasive. Distortion in stages (saturation on the kick, overdrive on the bus, clipping on the snare), gated reverbs, mono low end, metal hits bright and wide, vocals distorted and small.

## Stereo & space
- Width from the textures, not the groove: wide metallic resonances (stereo convolution, Haas on the pipe/anvil layers), factory ambience in stereo, noise sweeps panned opposite to the lead.
- Ping-pong delays on found-object perc (chain, hammer) at dotted-8th or 3/16, feedback 30-40%, highpassed at 400 Hz.
- Kick, rumble, sub and distorted bass strictly mono and centered; snare centered with its metallic layer slightly widened.
- Hard-panned found-object hits (pipe hard left, chain hard right) answering each other across bars; hats panned 30% one way with the open hat/steam burst on the other side.
- Gated reverbs instead of long tails: the room is big but it stops dead, which keeps the center dry.

## Common mistakes
- Clean kick (it must be distorted or clipped, with a rumble or an 808 under it); too much reverb (industrial is dry and gated, big tails make it dark techno); sounding like plain techno (no metal hits, no dropouts, no sampled speech, no distortion); no dynamics or dropouts (everything on for 4 minutes); no distortion staging (one giant distortion on the master instead of saturation on the kick, overdrive on the bass, clipping on the snare); hats too clean and bright; chords too present (industrial is a drone and a riff); swing (it is a machine, swing 0).
