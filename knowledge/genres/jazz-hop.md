# Jazz Hop / Jazzy Hip Hop (and the jazz language behind it)
slug: jazz-hop
aliases: jazz hop, jazz-hop, jazzhop, jazzy hip hop, jazz rap, jazz hip hop, nu jazz, nujazz, acid jazz, jazz beat, jazzy beat, jazz, swing beat, bebop hip hop
tempo: 82-96 (jazz hop), 120-200 (straight-ahead swing, for reference only)

## Summary
Jazz hop, 84-94 BPM, swing 0.2-0.35 (MPC 58-65%). Drums: dusty sampled kit, brushed or tight acoustic snare on 2 and 4, soft round kick, swung 16th hats w/ velocity 100-55 cycles, ride 8ths on the hook, rimshot ghosts. Kick: "soft round jazz kit kick from a 60s record, felt beater, 350 ms, tape, dry". Snare: "brushed jazz snare, crisp papery crack, short room, 250 ms". Pattern: kick 0:112 7:88 10:104 | snare 4:118 12:118 ghost 15:40 | hats swung 8ths 96/58 w/ 16th skips on 6, 14 | ride 8ths 80 on the hook. Bass: upright walking or 2-feel (roots + 5ths, chromatic approach notes), E1-G2, lands w/ the kick on 1 and walks between kicks; Live: Piano Bass, Fine Line Bass. Chords: 7ths/9ths/13ths on Rhodes or piano (ii7-V7-Imaj7, i7-iv7-VII7, modal m9/m11 vamps), rootless voicings, sampled horn phrases, vibes, muted trumpet. Tex: vinyl crackle, room tone, record stop/start, crowd murmur, spoken-word snippets. Form: 2-4 bar sample loop w/ a chopped variation every 8 bars, drums drop on the hook turnaround. Avoid: quantized straight hats, EDM-clean drums, triads only, a synth sub replacing the upright, loops that never breathe.

## Era & tempo
- Roots 1988-1996: A Tribe Called Quest, Gang Starr/DJ Premier, Digable Planets, Guru's Jazzmatazz, Pete Rock: sampled Blue Note/CTI records over 88-96 BPM boom bap. 1996-2006: Nujabes, Madlib/Yesterdays New Quintet, J Dilla's drunken swing, Hi-Tek, Common "Like Water": 82-94 BPM, looser timing. Lo-fi/jazz hop playlists 2015-now: 70-90 BPM, more chill (see lofi-hiphop). Straight-ahead swing itself runs 120-200 BPM and is a reference language, not the beat tempo.

- Hats: "TrackType: SFX, closed hi-hat played with a wooden stick tip, dark and dry, 60 ms, vinyl"; ride "TrackType: SFX, jazz ride cymbal ping with a soft wash, 20 inch, 700 ms decay, room"

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 90 BPM, swing 0.25-0.35)
1. Tribe/Premier: kick 0:112 7:88 10:104 | snare 4:118 12:118 | ghost snare 15:40 | hats swung 8ths 96/58, 16th skip at 6:50 14:50 | sidestick 11:60 in bar 2.
2. Dilla drunk: kick 0:118 6:90 10:100 (6 pushed 25 ms late, 10 pulled 15 ms early) | snare 4:115 12:110 (12 late 20 ms) | hats 16ths 90-50-70-45 heavily swung | no two bars identical.
3. Nujabes hook: kick 0:110 8:100 11:92 | snare 4:120 12:120 | ride 8ths 80/65 replaces closed hats | shaker 16ths 55 | crash 0 bar 1 of the hook.
4. 2-feel ballad: kick 0:100 8:96 | sidestick 4:95 12:95 | hats 8ths 70/50 | brush sweep on 2 and 4 under everything.

## Bass
- TYPE: upright/acoustic walking bass or "2-feel" (half notes on 1 and 3) with chromatic approach notes (a half step below/above the next root on the "and" of 4), occasionally a fingered electric (P-Bass with flatwounds). Register E1-G2. It lands with the kick on 1 and walks through the kick gaps; it never doubles a synth sub.
- Live (verified): Piano Bass (closest to upright), Fine Line Bass, Electric Bass Drive; add Compressor (slow attack) and roll off above 2 kHz.

## Harmony & keys
- 7th/9th/13th chords on Rhodes, acoustic piano, vibes, guitar: ii7-V7-Imaj7 (Dm7-G7-Cmaj7), minor ii-V-i (Dm7b5-G7alt-Cm9), modal vamps on a single m9 or m11 (Em11 for 4 bars), I-vi-ii-V turnarounds, borrowed bVII. Rootless left-hand voicings (3-7-9), right hand melody on the 9th/13th; keys Eb, Bb, F, Cm, Gm common from horn records.
- Instruments: Rhodes w/ chorus, upright piano w/ tape, vibraphone, muted trumpet, tenor sax phrase samples, nylon or hollow-body guitar comping, flute.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- A 2-bar sampled horn or vibes phrase answered by a chopped piano run; call-and-response between the sample and a rapped ad-lib; a bass lick fill into the hook; a sax phrase pitched to the key for the chorus; filter sweep on the sample for 4 bars before the drop.

## Textures & FX
- Vinyl crackle and needle drop, tape hiss, room tone between phrases, record stop (tape-stop) into the hook, scratched vocal snippet, spoken word/ interview snippets, rain/ city ambience (lo-fi side), reverse cymbal.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4 (sample alone, crackle) → verse 16 (full loop) → hook 8 (ride, horn phrase, extra chop) → verse 16 → hook 8 → bridge 8 (drums out, bass + Rhodes) → hook 8 → outro 4 (sample filters out). Variation every 8 bars via a different chop or a bar-4 fill.

## Mix signature
- Warm, mid-forward, drums dusty and slightly behind the sample in level; low end from the upright, not a sub; mono-ish center, light tape saturation on the bus.

## References (verify)
- A Tribe Called Quest "Electric Relaxation", Gang Starr "Mass Appeal", Guru "Loungin'", Pete Rock & CL Smooth "T.R.O.Y.", Nujabes "Feather" / "Aruarian Dance", Madlib "Slim's Return", J Dilla "Don't Cry", Common "The Light", Robert Glasper "Black Radio".

## Common mistakes
- Straight, quantized hats; a clean synth sub under an upright; only triads; the same bar looped for 32 bars with no chop variation; drums louder than the sample; swing applied to the kick too (keep the kick near the grid, swing the snare/hats).
