# Trap
slug: trap
aliases: trap, trap beat, atlanta trap, 808 trap, metro boomin, southside, type beat trap, modern rap beat, hard trap, melodic trap, rage
tempo: 130-170 (half-time feel; 65-85 effective)

## Summary
Trap, 130-170 BPM in half-time (snare on 3). Drums: clean punchy 808 kick, hard clap/snare w/ bright crack on beat 3, 16th hats w/ 32nd and triplet rolls, open hat on the "and", snaps/rim/perc fills. Kick: "tight modern trap kick, short punchy click, 120 ms, dry" (the 808 BASS carries the low end). Snare: "hard trap snare, layered clap, bright crack, short gated room, 200 ms". Pattern (half-time): kick 0:120 10:100 (b2: 0:120 6:90 11:100) | snare 8:127 (+ clap layer) | hats 16ths 100-60 cycle w/ 32nd roll on 14-15 and a triplet roll on bar 4 | open hat 14:90 | snap 8. Bass: 808 sub (sine/decaying saw, long tail, sat) tuned to the key, ONE instr w/ the kick: hits w/ the kick on 0 and 10, slides (glide) into the next root on bar ends, 1-3 notes per bar. Chords: dark minor keys (C#, F, G minor), 2-4 chord loops i-VI-VII or i-iv-v w/ 7ths; bells, plucks, piano, flute, choir, detuned pad; melody is a short 2-bar loop w/ counter-melody. Tex: risers, reverse crash, vocal chop tags, 808 glide, snap/rim fills. Form: 8 intro (melody), 16 verse, 8 hook w/ drop. Avoid: kick fighting the 808 (both w/ sub), hats all one vel, no rolls.

## Era & tempo
- 2010-now Atlanta lineage (Lex Luger, Southside/808 Mafia, Metro Boomin, Zaytoven, Mike WiLL). 130-170 BPM programmed in half-time: snare on beat 3 only, hats in 16ths/32nds read as fast against a slow bounce. Melodic trap 140-160, hard trap 140-150, rage/opium 150-170 with distorted 808s.

- Snare: "TrackType: SFX, hard trap snare layered with a tight clap, bright crack around 2 kHz, short gated room, 200 ms, compressed, dry"
- Clap: "TrackType: SFX, sharp layered clap, bright, 150 ms, slight reverb tail"
- Hats: "TrackType: SFX, clean closed hi-hat, tight metallic tick, 50 ms, dry"; open: "open hi-hat, bright sizzle, 300 ms"
- Perc: snaps, rim click, "tom" fills, cowbell (rage), percussion loops (shaker 16ths low), vocal chop "ay".

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps = 1 bar at 140-150 BPM, half-time)
1. Standard (Metro-style, 140-150): kick 0:120 10:100 | bar2 kick 0:120 6:88 11:100 | snare 8:127 every bar (clap layer 8:110) | hats 16ths 100-62-80-58 cycle | 32nd roll on 14-15 (vel 70→100 crescendo) bar 2 and 4 | triplet roll (3 per 8th) on 12-15 bar 4 | open hat 14:88 bars 2,4 | snap 8:70 with the snare.
2. Sparse/dark (Southside, 140): kick 0:127 7:110 | snare 8:127 | hats 8ths only 95/70 with a 32nd stutter on 15 | rim 4,12:60.
3. Melodic/bouncy (Zaytoven, 150-160): kick 0:118 5:95 10:110 13:90 | snare 8:125 | hats 16ths cycling 105-60-85-55 | open hat 6 and 14 | snap 4,12:80 (doubles as perc).
4. Rage (160-170): kick 0:127 (808 distorted) | snare 8:127 | hats 8ths 110/90 heavy | cowbell 2,6,10,14 | synth stabs on the kick.

## Bass
- TYPE: 808 sub bass as THE bass instrument: sine-based with harmonics (saturation/distortion for small speakers), 0.8-1.5 s decay, mono, tuned to the key; glide (portamento) between notes at phrase ends.
- Register: C1-A1 roots. Pattern: hits with the kick (0, 10), holds through, slides on the last 808 of a 2-bar phrase; 1-3 notes per bar; sometimes a quick 16th double before the snare.
- Live (verified): Sub808Bass, Synthetic Kick Bass Trap, 808 Pitch Boom; rage: Distorted Sine Bass; or Simpler on a tuned 808 one-shot (Classic mode, legato glide 60-120 ms); dirt: Saturator (Drive 12-20) or Overdrive.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor keys (C#m, Fm, Gm, Dm): 2-4 chord loops i-VI-VII (Fm-Db-Eb), i-iv-VII, i-v-VI; often a single minor vamp with a melodic lead.
- Instruments: bells/music box, plucks, piano (dark, reverb), flute/ocarina leads, choir/vox pads, detuned saw pads, guitar (melodic trap), brass stabs (hard trap).
- Voicings: melody in the 4th-5th octave, chords sparse below; 2-bar melodic loop + a counter-melody entering on the hook.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- A 2-bar lead phrase that repeats; octave-up variation on the hook; counter-melody; 808 slide as a hook device; call-and-response between lead and pluck; drop-out of melody on bar 1 of the verse.

## Textures & FX
- Risers (white noise, 2-4 bars) into drops, reverse crash/cymbal, downlifters, vocal chop tags, "ay/yeah" ad-libs, snap/rim fills, laser/zap FX (rage), sub drops at the hook.

## Arrangement
- Intro 8 (melody only, maybe a tag) → verse 16 (full drums, 808) → hook 8 (add counter-melody, open hats, maybe double-time hats) → verse 16 → hook 8 → bridge 8 (drums out, melody + 808) → hook 8 → outro 4. Drop-out of drums for the last bar before each hook; hat rolls mark bar 4 and 8.

## Mix signature
- 808 is the loudest element after the snare; kick is a click. Hats bright and panned slightly; lead melody wet (reverb/delay), drums dry. Huge mono low end.

## References (verify)
- Metro Boomin & Future "Mask Off", 21 Savage "Bank Account", Migos "Bad and Boujee", Travis Scott "SICKO MODE", Playboi Carti "Magnolia" (rage: "Stop Breathing"), Zaytoven/Gucci Mane, Lex Luger/Waka Flocka "Hard in da Paint".

## Common mistakes
- Snare on 2 and 4 at 140 (that becomes fast boom-bap); kick with its own sub colliding with the 808; hats at a constant velocity without rolls; 808 not tuned to the melody; melody filling every beat; no risers/transitions; no ad-libs; everything stereo-wide including the 808.
