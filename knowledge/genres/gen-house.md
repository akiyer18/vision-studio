# House (Chicago / Deep / Garage / French)
slug: gen-house
aliases: house, chicago house, deep house, classic house, garage house, new york garage, soulful house, french house, filter house, piano house, acid house, jackin house, disco house, tech house, 909 house
tempo: 118-128 (Chicago 118-126, deep 118-124, garage 120-125, French/filter 122-128, tech house 124-128)

## Summary
House, 120-126 BPM, swung 16ths (swing 0.10-0.20, MPC 54-58%). Drums: 909/808 kick four on the floor, clap on 2 and 4 (often layered w/ snare), offbeat open hat, swung 16th closed hats/shaker, congas/bongos, rim, tambourine. Pattern: kick 0,4,8,12:120 | clap 4,12:105 | open hat 2,6,10,14:95 | closed 16ths 80-50-65-50 swung | shaker 16ths 60 | conga 3,7,11:70. Bass: syncopated offbeat sub or organ bass (M1-style organ bass, sine/Juno sub, or rolling 16ths), root-fifth-octave movement, notes placed between kicks, F1-C3. Chords: minor 7th/9th/11th stabs, Rhodes or M1-style piano, i7-iv7 or ii-V loops, gospel/disco piano, string pads. Tex: vinyl crackle, filtered disco samples, vocal chops, stab delays, filter sweeps. Form: 8/16-bar phrases, 16-32 bar DJ intro/outro, breakdown w/ chords+vocal, drums return on bar 1. Avoid: dead quantized hats, bass stacked on the kick (mud), EDM supersaw drops with snare rolls.

## Era & tempo
- Chicago 1984-90 (Frankie Knuckles, Larry Heard/Mr. Fingers, Marshall Jefferson, Phuture, Farley "Jackmaster" Funk): 118-126, drum machines (808/909/707), raw, jackin, acid 303 lines.
- New York / New Jersey garage late 80s-90s (Larry Levan era, Masters at Work, Kerri Chandler, Todd Terry): 120-125, soulful vocals, organ bass, swung drums.
- Deep house (Larry Heard onward; Detroit: Moodymann, Theo Parrish): 118-124, jazzy chords, warm pads.
- French/filter house late 90s (Daft Punk, Stardust, Cassius): 122-128, filtered disco loops, compressed pumping.
- Tech house / modern 2010s-now: 124-128, tighter, percussive, rolling bass.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 124 BPM, swung 16ths)
1. Classic Chicago/jackin: kick 4otf 120 | clap 4,12:105 | open hat 2,6,10,14:95 | closed 16ths 80-50-65-50 swung | rim 7,15:70 | cowbell 3,11:60 bar 2.
2. Deep house: soft kick 4otf 110 | snare/clap 4,12:90 (snap layer) | open hat 2,6,10,14:80 | shaker 16ths 55-40 swung | conga 3,7,10,13:65 | ride 8ths low velocity.
3. Garage/soulful: kick 4otf 118 | clap 4,12:105 + tambourine 2,6,10,14:75 | closed hats 16ths 75-45 heavy swing | bongos 1,5,9,11,14:60 | clap ghost 15:50 bar 4.
4. Filter/French: kick 4otf 127 hard-compressed | clap 4,12:110 | open hat offbeats 100 | drums pumping with the sidechained sample loop, filter on the loop opening over 16 bars.
5. Breakdown: kick out 8-16 bars, chords + vocal + shaker; kick returns on bar 1 with a short noise sweep or a single clap pickup.

## Bass
- TYPE: (a) M1-style organ bass (short, percussive, offbeat syncopation, octave jumps) for garage/90s; (b) warm sine/triangle or Juno-style sub with offbeat notes (deep house); (c) rolling 16th bass, short plucky saw with lowpass (tech house); (d) acid 303 line (Chicago acid); (e) disco/funk bass sample, filtered (French house).
- Movement: avoid the kick steps; land on 2,6,10,14 or syncopated 3,7,11, root-octave-fifth, follows the chord roots, short decay so the kick breathes. Register F1-C3; sidechain lightly to the kick.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor keys dominate (Am, Fm, Gm, Cm, Ebm); extended chords: m7, m9, m11, maj7, dominant 9 and sus chords.
- Loops: i7-iv7 (Am7-Dm7), ii-V (Dm9-G13), i-VI-VII, vi-IV-I-V (soulful), single-chord vamp with a moving bassline.
- Instruments: Rhodes/electric piano, M1-style house piano (bright, stabby, gospel voicings), organ, string pads, warm analog pads, chord stabs (one-shot sampled chord played on different roots).

## Hooks & melodic devices
- Vocal hook (diva line or a chopped phrase repeated), piano riff on syncopated 8ths, the chord stab moving in parallel, filtered disco sample loop, a call-and-response between vocal and piano, acid line slowly opening, spoken-word vocal over a groove.

## Textures & FX
- Vinyl crackle and tape hiss, lowpass filter sweeps (open over 16-32 bars), stab delays (dotted 8th), plate/hall reverb on vocals, phaser on pads/hats, pumping sidechain compression, crowd/ambient room noise, reverse cymbals into sections.

## Arrangement
- 8/16-bar phrases, DJ-friendly: intro 16-32 (kick + hats/perc) → add bass 16 → chords/piano 16 → vocal/hook 32 → breakdown 16 (kick out, chords + vocal, filter) → main 32 → strip down → outro 16-32 drums only. Fills: clap rolls only 1 beat, perc drops, hat removal on bar 8.

## Mix signature
- Kick and bass interlocked (sidechain, bass between kicks), warm and round low end in mono, hats/perc crisp and slightly swung, chords mid-range with reverb, vocal on top; gentle bus compression for groove, not loud-at-all-costs.

## Common mistakes
- Fully quantized robotic hats (no swing), bass notes landing on the kick, too much low-mid mud from pads, EDM supersaw drops with long snare rolls, chords too simple (triads only), no percussion layer, overcompressed lifeless groove, structures too short for DJ mixing.
