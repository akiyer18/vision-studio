# Soul / R&B (90s and 2000s R&B production, with the neo-soul crossover)
slug: soul-rnb
aliases: r&b, rnb, r and b, rhythm and blues, soul music, soul track, soul song, 70s soul, classic soul, motown, 90s r&b, 2000s r&b, slow jam, quiet storm, new jack swing, contemporary r&b, modern r&b, alternative r&b, pbr&b, soul beat
tempo: 68-100 (slow jams 60-75, mid 85-95, new jack swing 105-115)

## Summary
R&B, 70-95 BPM, swing 0.15-0.3 (16ths 58-62%). Drums: programmed and polished: tight punchy kick (not an 808 sub), snare or rimshot on 2 and 4 layered w/ a clap or snap, swung 16th hats w/ 32nd stutters (Timbaland), finger snaps, shaker, tambourine; 2000s = sparser, more syncopated. Kick: "tight punchy R&B kick, controlled low end around 60 Hz, soft click, 300 ms, clean studio". Snare: "crisp R&B snare layered with a tight clap, bright crack, short plate, 220 ms". Pattern: kick 0:112 6:90 10:108 | snare+clap 4:120 12:120 | snaps 4,12 + 14:70 | hats swung 16ths 95-55-75-50 | shaker 16ths 50. Bass: warm fingered electric or soft synth bass (sine+triangle, slight glide), roots on the kick w/ a melodic pickup on the "and" of 4, A1-E3, under the Rhodes. Chords: Rhodes/piano w/ 7ths, 9ths, 11ths, 13ths; ii7-V7-Imaj7, Imaj7-vi7-ii7-V7, iv7-i7 vamps; sus chords, borrowed IV; keys Eb, Ab, Db, Bb, Cm, Fm. Hook: the vocal; instrumentals use a guitar lick, string/pad swell, chopped "oohs". Tex: snaps, string swells, airy pads, breathy vocal chops. Form: intro 4, verse 16, pre 8, chorus 8, bridge 8. Avoid: trap hats, an 808 as the bass, triads, hard-quantized grooves, drums crowding the vocal.

## Era & tempo
- New jack swing 1987-93 (Teddy Riley, Babyface): 105-115 BPM, swung hats, hard snare. 90s R&B (Jodeci, Mary J. Blige, Aaliyah, TLC): 85-100, hip hop drums + lush chords. Timbaland/Missy 1996-2003: stuttered hats, syncopated kicks, sparse arrangements, beatbox and odd sounds. Darkchild/Rodney Jerkins 1998-2004 ("Say My Name", "If You Had My Love"): staccato strings, tight guitar stabs, syncopated kicks, 95-105. Neo-soul 1996-2005 (D'Angelo, Erykah Badu, Musiq): 70-90 looser (see neo-soul). 2010s alt R&B (Frank Ocean, SZA, The Weeknd, H.E.R.): 70-90, airy, 808 sub acceptable, lo-fi edges.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 90 BPM, swing 0.2-0.3)
1. Classic mid-tempo: kick 0:112 6:90 10:108 | snare+clap 4:120 12:120 | snaps 4:80 12:80 14:65 | hats swung 16ths 95-55-75-50 | shaker 16ths 50.
2. Timbaland syncopation: kick 0:118 3:95 10:110 13:88 | snare 4:120 12:115 | hats 8ths 90/60 w/ 32nd stutter at 7 and 15 | beatbox pop 5:70 | tambourine 2,6,10,14:60.

## Bass
- TYPE: warm round fingered electric bass (flatwound, little top) or a soft synth bass (sine + triangle, slight glide, 60-250 Hz); roots on the kick, melodic pickup (5th or chromatic approach) on the "and" of 4 into the next chord, occasional octave pop on beat 3; never busier than the kick. 2010s alt R&B: a clean 808 sub on roots is acceptable, long notes.
- Live (verified): Fine Line Bass, Mello Bass, Electric Bass Drive; synth side: Smoked Analog Bass, Hip-Hop Sub Bass (alt R&B).

## Harmony & keys
- Extended chords: 7ths, 9ths, 11ths, 13ths, sus4/sus2; progressions ii7-V7-Imaj7, Imaj7-vi7-ii7-V7, iv7-i7 (minor vamp), bVImaj7-bVII7-i; modal interchange (borrowed IVm). Rootless voicings (3-7-9-13) on Rhodes/EP, left-hand roots sparse; top note of the voicing moves stepwise. Keys Eb, Ab, Db, Bb, Cm, Fm, Gm.
- Instruments: Rhodes/Wurli w/ chorus, grand piano, acoustic and muted electric guitar licks, strings (staccato for Darkchild, swells for ballads), airy pads, bells.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The vocal is the hook; instrumental hooks: a guitar lick answering the vocal, a 2-bar string riff, chopped "ooh" vocal pads, a bell/EP motif on the chorus top, a pre-chorus riser (reverse cymbal + string swell), drum dropout on the last bar before the chorus.

## Textures & FX
- Finger snaps, string swells, airy breath pads, chopped vocal "oohs/aahs" (pitched within ±3 st), vinyl only for neo-soul, tape stop into the bridge, reverse reverb on the snare for the chorus, triangle/shaker for air.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4 (chords + hook motif) → verse 16 (sparse drums, bass) → pre-chorus 8 (snaps, strings) → chorus 8 (full kit, claps, pads) → verse 16 → pre 8 → chorus 8 → bridge 8 (new chord, drums half) → chorus 8 (ad-libs) → outro 4. Fills: snare roll or hat stutter on bar 8/16, drum dropout on the last beat before the chorus.

## Mix signature
- Vocal space first: drums tight and polished but not loud, bass warm and controlled, Rhodes wide w/ chorus, pads behind, snaps crisp on top; light bus glue.

## Common mistakes
- Trap hats and 808 slides in a 90s/2000s R&B brief; triads instead of 7ths/9ths; hard quantization (R&B breathes: swing the hats, keep the kick tight); a kick with too much sub fighting the bass; no snaps/claps layered on the backbeat; arrangement without a pre-chorus lift.
