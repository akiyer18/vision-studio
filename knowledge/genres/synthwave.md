# Synthwave / Retrowave
slug: synthwave
aliases: synthwave, retrowave, outrun, 80s synth, darksynth, vaporwave (adjacent), chillwave (adjacent), kavinsky, the midnight, 80s retro beat, linndrum
tempo: 80-118 (outrun 100-118, ballads 80-95, darksynth 120-140)

## Summary
Synthwave, 100-112 BPM, straight (swing 0). Drums are 80s drum machines: LinnDrum/DMX/Simmons: punchy dry kick w/ short click, gated-reverb snare (big, cut-off tail) on 2 and 4, bright 8th hats, toms w/ gated reverb for fills, claps layered. Kick: "LinnDrum-style 80s kick, punchy, short, dry, 200 ms". Snare: "80s gated reverb snare, big and bright w/ tail cut after 250 ms, Simmons-like crack". Pattern: kick 0:118 8:118 (variant 0,6,8,10) | snare 4:125 12:125 (clap layer 4,12:90) | hats 8ths 100/75 (16ths at 80/55 in choruses) | tom fill 12-15 every 8 bars | crash on section starts. Bass: analog synth bass (saw/square, Juno/Moog-style) playing driving 8th or 16th notes on the root w/ octave jumps (0 and +12 alternating), ducked to the kick; the "outrun" pulse. Chords: minor and major w/ 80s pop changes: i-VI-III-VII, vi-IV-I-V, I-V-vi-IV; big Juno/Jupiter pads (chorus), poly-synth chord stabs, arpeggios (16ths), FM bells, brass leads, lead w/ portamento and vibrato; keys Am, Em, F#m, C, D. Form: 8 intro, 16 verse, 8 pre, 16 chorus, solo, outro. Avoid: swing, modern trap hats, no gated snare.

## Era & tempo
- Revival of 1980s film/pop production (Harold Faltermeyer, Jan Hammer, Tangerine Dream, Giorgio Moroder) by Kavinsky, Perturbator, The Midnight, Gunship, FM-84, Timecop1983. 100-112 BPM outrun; ballads 80-95; darksynth 120-140.

## Drum sounds (Stable Audio-ready)
- Kick: "TrackType: SFX, LinnDrum-style 1980s drum machine kick, punchy with a short click, 200 ms, dry one-shot"
- Snare: "TrackType: SFX, 1980s gated reverb snare, big bright crack with a huge reverb cut off abruptly after 250 ms, Simmons-like, compressed"
- Clap: "TrackType: SFX, 80s drum machine clap with gated reverb, 220 ms"
- Hats: closed "TrackType: SFX, bright 80s drum machine closed hi-hat, 70 ms, crisp"; open "open hat, bright, 300 ms"
- Toms: "TrackType: SFX, Simmons electronic tom with pitch drop and gated reverb, 300 ms"; crash cymbal, orchestra hit, cowbell (DMX).

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 108 BPM, straight)
1. Outrun drive: kick 0:118 8:118 (bar2 0:118 6:95 8:118 10:90) | snare 4:125 12:125 + clap 4,12:90 | hats 8ths 100/75 | open hat 14:85 bar 4 | tom fill 12:95 13:100 14:105 15:110 every 8 bars | crash 0 at sections.
2. Mid-tempo ballad (90): kick 0:115 10:95 | snare 8:122 (half-time) | hats 8ths 85/60 | tambourine 8 | big tom fill every 8.
3. Darksynth (128): kick 4otf 125 | snare 4,12:127 | hats 16ths 95-70 | distorted bass stabs with the kick.
4. Chorus lift: hats to 16ths 80/55, add ride 8ths, snare + clap + crash.

## Bass
- TYPE: analog synth bass (saw or square, lowpass 400-800 Hz with a little envelope, slight chorus), Moog/Juno-style; sidechain pump to the kick. Register A1-E2. Pattern: driving 8ths on the root (all steps 0,2,4,...,14 at 100/85) or 16ths with octave alternation (root, +12, root, +12...), following the chord changes; a 16th pickup into each bar.
- Live (verified): June Oh Bass, JX Chorus Bass, Analog Saw Bass, Analogue Bass; Chorus light; Compressor (no sidechain API: shorten notes after the kick to emulate the pump).

## Harmony & keys
- 80s pop harmony: i-VI-III-VII (Am-F-C-G), vi-IV-I-V (F#m-D-A-E), I-V-vi-IV, with sus4 and add9 color; 1 chord per bar, pads sustained, stabs on 2 and 4 or the "and" of 2.
- Instruments: Juno/Jupiter-style poly pads (chorus, slow attack), poly stabs (brass-like saw), 16th arpeggios (pluck, delay 1/8 dotted), FM/DX7 bells and e-pianos, brass lead (saw, portamento, vibrato), sax-like lead, electric guitar (clean chorus or distorted solo).
- Voicings: pads in root position mid register, stabs in close voicing, arpeggio spanning two octaves, lead an octave above.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The arpeggio (16ths, filter opening); a brass/lead melody with portamento; a chord stab rhythm; octave bass jump into the chorus; tom fill + crash into each section; key change (+1 or +2 semitones) for the last chorus.

## Textures & FX
- Gated reverb on snare/toms/claps, long hall on pads, tape saturation/wow, vinyl (vaporwave), neon risers, reverse reverb swells, orchestra hits, white-noise sweeps, laser/zap FX, vocoder/talkbox.

## Arrangement
- Intro 8 (pad + arp) → verse 16 (drums, bass) → pre 8 (build hats, stabs) → chorus 16 (lead, full) → verse 16 → chorus 16 → bridge/solo 16 (guitar/lead) → chorus 16 (+key change) → outro 8. Fills every 8 bars with toms.

## Mix signature
- Bright and wide, pads huge with chorus, bass mono and pumping, snare enormous but short (gate), drums dry except the gate, lead forward.

## References (verify)
- Kavinsky "Nightcall", The Midnight "Sunset", Perturbator "Future Club" (darksynth), Gunship "Tech Noir", FM-84 "Running in the Night", Timecop1983, Harold Faltermeyer "Axel F", Jan Hammer "Crockett's Theme".

## Common mistakes
- Swing on the drums, trap hats, no gated snare, static bass (needs 8th/16th pulse), pads without chorus/width, no arpeggio, modern sub-heavy kick, no tom fills/crashes, lead without portamento/vibrato.
