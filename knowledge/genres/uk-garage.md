# UK Garage / 2-Step
slug: uk-garage
aliases: uk garage, ukg, 2-step, two step, 2 step garage, speed garage, garage, mj cole, todd edwards, el-b, bassline (adjacent), skippy drums
tempo: 128-135

## Summary
UK garage, 130-134 BPM, shuffle swing 0.35-0.5 (16ths pushed late), skippy "2-step" kick that skips beat 3. Drums: tight snappy kick, crisp snare+clap on 2 and 4 (often a layered rimshot/snare w/ pitch), swung 16th hats w/ open hats on skips, shuffle shakers, pitched vocal chops as perc, perc hits (woodblock, conga). Kick: "tight snappy UK garage kick, short punch, 250 ms, dry". Snare: "crisp garage snare layered w/ rimshot and clap, snappy, 180 ms, light plate". Pattern (2-step): kick 0:120 10:105 (b2: 0:120 7:95 10:105) | snare 4:118 12:118 | hats swung 16ths 95-55-80-50 w/ open hat 7 and 15 | shaker swung 16ths 45 | rim 3:70 14:65 | vocal chop 6:90. Bass: deep warm sub/reese-lite (sine + slight detune) playing sync'd 8ths/16ths that ANSWER the kick (steps 2-3, 6, 11, 14), plus a speed-garage "warp" bass for 4x4; 2-4 notes per bar, slides. Chords: soulful R&B chords: m7/m9/maj7, ii-V-I in major/minor (Ab, Bb, Eb keys), Rhodes/organ stabs on offbeats, string pads, pitched-up vocal samples. Form: 16 intro, 16 verse, 8-16 hook, breakdown, hook. Avoid: straight hats (must swing), kick on every beat (that's 4x4 house), no vocal chops.

## Era & tempo
- 1995-2003 London (Todd Edwards influence, MJ Cole, Artful Dodger, El-B, Zed Bias), revived 2015-now (Conducta, Interplanetary Criminal). 128-135 BPM, swing heavy, pitched vocal chops, R&B harmony, bass from house/jungle. 2-step skips the kick on beat 3; 4x4/speed garage keeps four kicks with a warped bass.

## Drum sounds (Stable Audio-ready)
- Kick: "TrackType: SFX, tight snappy UK garage kick drum, short punch with a soft knock, 250 ms, dry one-shot"
- Snare: "TrackType: SFX, crisp UK garage snare layered with a rimshot and a clap, snappy, 180 ms, light plate reverb"; pitched variant up 2-4 semitones for the hook
- Hats: closed "TrackType: SFX, crisp closed hi-hat with a shuffle feel, 55 ms"; open "short open hat, bright, 200 ms"
- Perc: shaker swung 16ths, woodblock, conga, rim click, finger snap, vocal chop one-shots (pitched "ooh", "yeah").

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 132 BPM, swing 0.4 on hats/shaker/perc)
1. 2-step: kick 0:120 10:105 | bar2 kick 0:120 7:95 10:105 | snare 4:118 12:118 (clap layer) | hats swung 16ths 95-55-80-50 | open hat 7:85 15:90 | shaker 16ths 45 | rim 3:70 14:65 | vocal chop 6:90 (bar 2: 13:85).
2. Skippy (El-B): kick 0:120 6:90 11:108 | snare 4:120 12:120 + rim 9:60 | hats 8ths 100/60 w/ swung 16th on 3,11 | snare roll 14:80 15:95 bar 4.
3. 4x4 / speed garage: kick 4otf 118 | clap 4,12 | open hat 2,6,10,14 | warp bass 16ths | vocal stabs.
4. Breakdown: drums to shaker + vocal chops; snare pitched up re-enters alone for 2 bars before the drop.

## Bass
- TYPE: deep warm sub with character (sine + a slightly detuned saw layer lowpassed, "reese-lite"), or a speed-garage "warp" bass (pitch-bent saw, 4x4 only). Register F1-C2. Pattern: syncopated, answering the kick: 2:100 3:85 | 6:95 | 11:100 | 14:90 variants, slides into bar 2; 2-4 notes per bar, never on every kick.
- Live (verified): Deep Wave Bass, Boom Sub Bass, Rump Shaker Bass; speed-garage warp: Wobble Bass; add Chorus lightly for reese-lite; Auto Filter lowpass 600 Hz.

## Harmony & keys
- Soulful R&B: m7, m9, maj7, dominant 7 sus, ii7-V7-Imaj7 (Bbm7-Eb7-Abmaj7), i-VI-VII in minor; keys Ab, Eb, Bb major and their relative minors.
- Voicings: Rhodes/organ stabs on offbeats (steps 2, 6 or 10), string pads, piano chords on the drop; pitched-up vocal sample singing the hook melody.
- Instruments: Rhodes, organ, strings, piano, plucks (speed garage), vocal samples (pitched +3 to +7 semitones, formant-shifted).

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The chopped vocal (time-stretched, pitched, rearranged syllables) is THE hook; a Rhodes/organ stab riff; snare pitched up on the hook; bass riff variation; the 2-step kick switching to 4x4 for 2 bars.

## Textures & FX
- Reverse snare/vocal into phrases, filtered intro (lowpass), tape stop, airhorn (rare), noise riser (short), vinyl crackle (light), delay throws on vocal chops.

## Arrangement
- Intro 16 (drums + chops) → verse 16 (bass in) → hook 16 (vocal + chords) → breakdown 8-16 → hook 16 → outro 16. Fills every 8: snare roll or vocal stutter on bar 8.

## Mix signature
- Snare bright and forward, hats wide and swung, sub mono and deep, vocal chops wet (plate/delay), chords mid and warm.

## References (verify)
- MJ Cole "Sincere", Artful Dodger "Re-Rewind", Todd Edwards "Saved My Life", El-B "Buck & Bury", Zed Bias "Neighbourhood", Conducta, Interplanetary Criminal "B.O.T.A.".

## Common mistakes
- Straight hats and shakers; kick on every beat in a 2-step; no vocal chops; bass doubling the kick; a modern clean snare without rim/clap/pitch; triad pads instead of 7th/9th chords; no snare pitch play.
