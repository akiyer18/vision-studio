# Techno (Berlin / Detroit)
slug: techno
aliases: techno, berlin techno, detroit techno, industrial techno, peak time techno, hard techno, minimal techno, dub techno, berghain, 909 techno, rumble
tempo: 128-140 (Detroit 125-135, Berlin 130-138, hard 140-150)

## Summary
Techno, 130-136 BPM, straight (swing 0 to 0.05). Drums: 909-style kick w/ rumble/sub tail (Berlin) or a tight punchy kick (Detroit), offbeat open hats, 16th closed hats w/ accents, clap or snare on 2 and 4 (often only a clap on 4, or none), rides on the peak, metallic perc and clicks. Kick: "Berlin techno kick, 909-style punch w/ long dist'd rumble tail, 60 Hz, 600 ms, sat'd, dry". Pattern: kick 0,4,8,12:127 | clap 4,12:100 (or 12 only) | open hat 2,6,10,14:100 | closed 16ths 85-55-70-55 | ride 8ths on peak | perc click 3,11:60 | tom 7:70. Bass: sub tied to the kick (the kick's rumble IS the bass) plus a one-note offbeat "rolling" bass or an acid line (303-ish saw w/ filter); Detroit = melodic synth bass octaves. Chords: minimal/atonal: one chord or a stab (minor, w/ dark pad), hypnotic 1-2 note sequences, acid lines, Detroit strings/chords (Fm, Dm). Tex: reverb washes, rumble, metallic hits, noise sweeps, vocal snippets pitched down, delays. Form: 32-bar phrases, long builds, breakdown w/ filtered kick, 8-bar fills. Avoid: swing/groove too loose, melodies and chords too present, kick without rumble, EDM drops, too many elements (techno is 5-7 very tight elements).

## Era & tempo
- Detroit 1985-95 (Juan Atkins, Derrick May, Jeff Mills, UR): 125-135, 909/808, strings, melodic. Berlin/Berghain 2005-now (Ben Klock, Marcel Dettmann, Len Faki; industrial: Ancient Methods, Perc): 128-138, rumble kicks, hypnotic loops, long forms. Hard/peak 2020s: 140-150.

## Drum sounds (Stable Audio-ready)
- Kick (Berlin): "TrackType: SFX, Berlin techno kick drum, 909-style punch with a long distorted sub rumble tail, 60 Hz, 600 ms decay, saturated, dry one-shot"
- Kick (Detroit): "TrackType: SFX, tight punchy 909 kick, short click, 250 ms, clean"
- Clap: "TrackType: SFX, 909 clap, sharp, with a big dark reverb tail, 400 ms"; snare "909 snare, snappy, 150 ms"
- Hats: closed "TrackType: SFX, 909 closed hi-hat resampled through a 12-bit sampler, crunchy, 50 ms"; open "909 open hat, metallic sizzle, 300 ms"
- Perc: metallic hits (pipe, anvil), rim clicks, toms (low, pitched), ride cymbal 8ths, shaker (minimal), noise bursts.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 132 BPM, straight)
1. Berlin hypnotic: kick 4otf 127 (rumble tail) | clap 12:100 only (sometimes 4,12) | open hat 2,6,10,14:100 | closed 16ths 85-55-70-55 | perc click 3:60 11:60 | low tom 7:70 bar 2 | ride 8ths on peak bars.
2. Detroit: kick 4otf 120 | clap/snare 4,12:110 | open hat 2,6,10,14:95 | closed 16ths 80-50 | hand clap double on 15 bar 4 | strings stab 0.
3. Industrial: kick 4otf 127 distorted | NO clap; metallic hit 4,12:110 | closed hats 16ths 90-60 (crunchy) | noise burst 14-15 every 4 bars | rumble constant.
4. Breakdown: kick filtered/removed 16 bars, hats + rumble + pad; kick returns with a long riser, no snare roll.

## Bass
- TYPE: (a) the kick's rumble as the bass (layered sub/reverb tail, sidechained), plus (b) a rolling one-note offbeat bass (short saw/square, steps 2,6,10,14) or (c) an acid 303 line (saw, resonant lowpass, accents/slides, 16th sequence) or (d) Detroit melodic synth bass (octave 8ths). Register F1-C2.
- Live (verified): rumble = Simpler with the kick tail + Reverb 100% wet lowpassed at 150 Hz; bass: Tough Electro Bass, Ostbahnhof, Boom Sub Bass; acid line: Reso Blaster, Zapp Bass.

## Harmony & keys
- Mostly atonal/one-key: a single minor stab (Fm, Dm, Am) with a long reverb, a 1-2 note hypnotic sequence (16th arpeggio with filter movement), Detroit: minor 7th string chords (i-VI, i-VII), pads, bell-like FM leads.
- Instruments: dark saw/square stabs, 303 acid, FM bells, strings (Detroit), noise textures, pitched toms as melody.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The hypnotic sequence (one motif, filter opening over 16-32 bars), a stab on the "and" of 2 every other bar, a vocal snippet pitched down, a riser into the drop, the rumble dropping out for 2 beats.

## Textures & FX
- Big dark reverb washes, delay feedback throws, white/pink noise sweeps (long), metallic clanks, tape saturation, sub drops, reverse reverb into the kick return.

## Arrangement
- 32-bar phrases: intro 32 (kick+hats+rumble) → 32 add perc/stab → 32 add sequence → breakdown 16-32 (kick out, pad/sequence, riser) → peak 64 → outro 32. Fills: perc flurries on bar 8/16, not snare rolls.

## Mix signature
- Kick + rumble = 60% of the energy, hats crisp and panned, stabs drenched in reverb, everything else small; mono low end, saturated bus.

## References (verify)
- Ben Klock "Subzero", Marcel Dettmann, Len Faki "Rainbow Delta", Jeff Mills "The Bells", Derrick May "Strings of Life", Robert Hood, Ancient Methods, Blawan "Why They Hide Their Bodies".

## Common mistakes
- Swing, chords and melodies in front, kick without rumble/sub, EDM build-drop with snare rolls, too many sounds, short 8-bar structures, bright happy stabs, reverb on the kick itself instead of a parallel rumble.
