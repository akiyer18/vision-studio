# 2000s Hip Hop (East Coast, West Coast G-funk, Dirty South/crunk, Timbaland/Neptunes)
slug: hiphop-2000s
aliases: 2000s hip hop, 2000s hiphop, 00s hip hop, y2k rap, early 2000s rap, 2000s rap, g-funk, g funk, dr dre 2001, dirty south, crunk, snap music, timbaland, neptunes, pharrell beat, radio rap 2000s, bling era
tempo: 88-100 (East/West), 70-82 crunk (double-time feel), 92-105 Timbaland/Neptunes

## Summary
2000s hip hop, 88-100 BPM (crunk 70-82). Drums are PROGRAMMED, not chopped breaks: fat 808-ish or Linn-style kick w/ knock, cracking snare layered w/ clap/snap on 2 and 4, dry 16th hats w/ few 32nd stutters, tambourine or snap doubling the backbeat. Kick: "2000s hip hop kick, round 808 body ~55 Hz w/ hard MPC click, 450 ms decay, dry". Snare: "big cracking layered snare, short room, bright crack, 300 ms". Pattern (G-funk bounce): kick 0:120 7:95 10:112 | snare+clap 4:120 12:120 | hats 8ths 100/70 w/ 16th pickup on 14-15 | snaps on 4,12. Bass: synth bass (Moog/Minimoog-style portamento or sine-sub 808) playing roots + octave slides, landing w/ the kick on 1 and answering it on the "and" of 2/3, long notes in G-funk, staccato in crunk. Chords: minor, sparse, 2-4 chords, whiny high lead (whistle/portamento square), piano stabs, strings pads, talkbox; Neptunes = dry Triton-style plastic synths and clave/woodblock perc w/ huge space. Tex: snaps, vocal "yeah/uh" ad-libs, reverse cymbal into the hook, vinyl only for East Coast. Form: 4 intro, 16 verse, 8 hook w/ drop-outs on the hook downbeat. Avoid: boom-bap swing (2000s is tighter), clean modern trap hats, no bass.

## Era & tempo
- 1999-2007: the shift from chopped breaks to programmed drums (MPC2000XL/3000, Triton, Motif, ASR-10 in the South) and from vinyl loops to played/synth "original composition" (Timbaland, Neptunes, Dre's live replays). Radio singles 88-100 BPM; crunk/Dirty South 70-82 BPM felt in double time (hats at 16ths read as 8ths); Timbaland/Neptunes 92-105 with heavy syncopation.
- Four sub-flavours below. The headline rule for all: tight programmed drums, a clear bass instrument that is a melody, a short repeating hook figure, lots of space.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps per bar; step:velocity)
1. G-funk bounce (Dre/Snoop/Nate Dogg feel, 90-96 BPM, swing 0.08):
   kick 0:120 7:95 10:112 (bar 2: 0:120 6:90 10:112 13:80) | snare 4:120 12:120 | clap doubles snare at 4,12 (vel 105) | hats 8ths 100/68, 16ths on 14,15 at 75/85 every other bar | tambourine 4,12 | open hat 14 every 4th bar.
2. East Coast 2000s (Just Blaze/Kanye soul-flip era, 88-94, swing 0.12):
   kick 0:118 3:70 6:100 10:110 | snare 4:120 12:120 (ghost 11:45) | hats 16ths cycling 100-62-82-58 | crash on 0 of the hook only.
3. Crunk (Lil Jon, 74-80 BPM, straight, programmed at half-time):
   kick 0:127 6:100 8:110 (long 808) | snare 8:127 (half-time backbeat) + snaps 8 and 12:90 | hats 16ths with 32nd stutters on 14-15 (vel 90-110) | brass stab on 0.
4. Timbaland syncopation (92-100, straight):
   kick 0:115 5:100 11:105 | snare 4:120 12:120 with extra snare 9:95 (bar 2) | hats on 8ths with a 16th ghost after each (100/55), occasional beatbox-like mouth clicks | woodblock 2,10.
5. Neptunes minimal (Grindin'/Drop It Like It's Hot, 94-100, straight): kick 0:120 10:118 | clap 4:120 12:120 (reverb tail) | tongue-click perc 2,6,14 | NO continuous hats, maybe 8th rimclick; the gaps are the point.

## Bass
- TYPE: synth bass. G-funk = Minimoog/Moog-style portamento bass (sawtooth, lowpass ~300 Hz, slow glide) playing the root and octave, or a sine/808 sub doubling it. Dirty South = long 808 tuned to the key (kick and bass are one instrument). East Coast = sampled upright/electric bass from the flip or a Rhodes bass. Timbaland/Neptunes = short plucky FM/Triton bass or deep sine stabs.
- Register: sub to low (E1-A1 root, octave up for movement). Pattern vs kick: hit WITH the kick on beat 1, then answer the kick on the "and" of 2 and the "and" of 3; hold long notes in G-funk (1-2 beats), staccato 8ths in Neptunes; slide into the root on the hook.
- Live presets (verified): Hip-Hop Sub Bass, Boom Sub Bass; G-funk Moog-style: Dank Moug, MINI 3OSC, Muugy, Funk Saw Bass; crunk long 808: Sub808Bass (or Simpler on a tuned sub one-shot with legato glide).

## Harmony & keys
- Minor keys (A, G, F#, E minor), 2-4 chord loops: i-VI-VII (Am-F-G), i-iv (Am-Dm), i-VI-III-VII, or a single minor vamp with a melodic lead.
- Voicings: sparse, mid-register; piano stabs on the "and" of 2 (East Coast), sustained strings pads (West), brass/synth-brass hits (crunk), bell/music-box or whistle lead an octave above (G-funk "whine": portamento square or sine lead, slow vibrato).
- Instruments: Rhodes, grand/upright piano stabs, strings (sampled orchestral), synth brass, whistle/portamento lead, talkbox, choir "aah" pad, pizzicato, harpsichord/music box (Timbaland), bubbly Triton plucks and bell tones (Neptunes).

## Hooks & melodic devices
- 2-bar repeating melodic figure that enters on the hook and drops out for the verse's first bar; call-and-response between lead and bass; octave jumps; a whistle/high lead "answering" the vocal gap; stop-time on the last bar before the hook (drums out, one 808 hit).

## Textures & FX
- Snaps and claps layered on the backbeat; tambourine; "yeah/uh/what" ad-lib samples on the 4th beat; reverse cymbal into the hook; DJ scratch stabs (East); vinyl crackle only for soul-flip East Coast; phone/beep/cartoon FX as percussion (Timbaland); huge gated reverb on claps (Neptunes); risers are rare, use drum fills and drop-outs instead.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4 bars (hook melody or vocal tag) → verse 16 → hook 8 → verse 16 → hook 8 → bridge/breakdown 8 → hook 8 → outro 4. Fills every 4 bars (hat stutter or snare roll on bar 4 steps 12-15), full drop-out on the first beat of each hook, bass solo for 1 bar before the last hook.

## Mix signature
- Drums loud and dry, bass as loud as the kick, lead melody above everything, vocal space in 1-4 kHz. Little reverb except snare/clap.

## Common mistakes
- Boom-bap swing and dusty breaks (that is 90s); hats too busy and bright like 2010s trap; no distinct bass instrument; chords too jazzy; missing the snap/clap/tambourine layer; no hook figure; everything on the grid at the same velocity; kick without the click; snare without a clap; melodic elements filling every gap (2000s beats breathe).
