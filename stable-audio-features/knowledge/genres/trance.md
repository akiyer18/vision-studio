# Trance (uplifting, progressive, psy edges)
slug: trance
aliases: trance, uplifting trance, progressive trance, vocal trance, psytrance, psy trance, goa, euphoric trance, 138, armin style, above and beyond style, anjunabeats style, tech trance, hard trance
tempo: 132-140 (uplifting 136-140, progressive 128-134, psy 140-148, hard 145-150)

## Summary
Trance, 138 BPM (prog 130), straight. Drums: tight 909-style kick w/ a clean sub (300-400 ms), clap + snare on 2 and 4 w/ a bright reverb, open hat on offbeats, 16th closed hats 90-55, ride on the peak, snare rolls (8ths to 16ths to 32nds over 8-16 bars) into the drop, crash every 8/16, noise risers. Kick: "uplifting trance kick, tight 909-style punch with a clean short sub, 350 ms, bright click, dry". Clap: "trance clap with a bright plate reverb, 400 ms". Pattern: kick 0,4,8,12:127 | clap 4,12:115 | open hat 2,6,10,14:105 | closed 16ths 90-55-75-50 | bass offbeat 8ths 2,6,10,14. Bass: the rolling offbeat bass: short saw/square on the "and"s (steps 2, 6, 10, 14) or psy 16ths, sidechained hard to the kick, one root per 1-2 bars following the chords, A1-A2. Chords: minor, emotional (i-VI-III-VII, i-VII-VI-VII), supersaw stacks (root-5th-octave + 3rd/7th), 16th pluck arps, an 8-16 bar lead melody w/ octave jumps and long notes; keys Am, Fm, Gm, Em, Dm. Hook: the drop lead, the pluck arp, the breakdown chord swell w/ piano, roll + riser + key lift into the drop. Tex: white noise, risers, gated pads, reverse crashes, delayed vocal, filter sweeps. Form: intro 32, break 32 (chords.

## Era & tempo
- 1993-1998 German/Goa (Jam & Spoon, Age of Love, Paul van Dyk "For an Angel"): 135-145. Uplifting 1998-2004 (Tiësto, Armin van Buuren, Ferry Corsten, Above & Beyond, Rank 1 "Airwave"): 136-140, supersaws, big breakdowns. Progressive trance 2005-2015 (Anjunabeats, Markus Schulz, Gareth Emery): 128-134, deeper, longer. Psytrance (Astrix, Vini Vici) 140-148, rolling 16th basslines. 2016-now: uplifting revival at 138, "trance 2.0" edges w/ future bass.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 138 BPM, straight)
1. Uplifting drop: kick 0,4,8,12:127 | clap 4,12:115 | open hat 2,6,10,14:105 | closed 16ths 90-55-75-50 | ride 8ths 70 | crash 0 bar 1 of 8 | bass offbeat 2,6,10,14 sidechained.
2. Progressive: kick 4otf 122 | clap 4,12:105 | open hat offbeats 95 | closed 16ths 75-45 | shaker 16ths 55 | bass 16ths 1-3-1-3 pattern (steps 1,3,5,7,...) sidechained | perc click 11:60.
3. Psy: kick 4otf 127 | no clap (or 12 only) | closed hats 16ths 85-60 | open hat offbeats 90 | rolling bass 16ths on all off-kick steps (1,2,3,5,6,7,...) | zap FX every 2 bars.
4. Build 16 bars: kick continues 8 bars then drops out; snare 8ths (bars 1-8) → 16ths (9-12) → 32nds (13-16) w/ rising pitch; riser; chords swell; 1 bar of silence or a reverse crash, then the drop w/ a crash.

## Bass
- TYPE: the offbeat/rolling bass: short saw/square (or psy: a distorted saw w/ a resonant filter) playing roots on the "and"s (2, 6, 10, 14) at 138, or 16th patterns (prog: 1-3-1-3; psy: every non-kick 16th), one root per 1-2 bars following the chords, sidechained hard (attack 0, release 120-200 ms), highpassed at 40 Hz, a sub layer under it. Register A1-A2.
- Live (verified): Analog Saw Bass, Tough Electro Bass, Bass Groove (synced, hold notes), Rump Shaker Bass; sidechain via Compressor keyed to the kick.

## Harmony & keys
- Minor, emotional: i-VI-III-VII (Am-F-C-G), i-VII-VI-VII, i-iv-VI-V, i-VI-VII (3-chord), vi-IV-I-V in the relative major for uplifting; 8-bar progressions in the break w/ a half-step key lift into the drop (uplifting cliché). Voicings: supersaw stacks (root-5th-octave + 3rd/7th), piano in the break (arpeggiated 8ths), plucks arpeggiating 16ths (1-5-8-10), the lead in octaves w/ long notes and leaps. Keys Am, Fm, Gm, Em, Dm, Bm.
- Instruments: supersaw lead (7+ voices, 3-5 layers highpassed, noise layer, high-cut reverb), plucks, piano, strings/gated pads, choir pad, acid-ish bass (tech trance), vocoder.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The drop lead melody (8-16 bars, long notes + octave jumps + a 16th run into bar 8), the pluck arpeggio under it, the breakdown piano stating the melody first, the snare roll + riser + key lift, a vocal phrase (female, delayed) in the break, a drop 2 w/ the lead an octave up and a counter-melody.

## Textures & FX
- White-noise risers/downlifters, reverse crashes into every 8/16, gated pads (sidechained to a 16th pulse), long delays (dotted 8th) on the lead, shimmer reverb on plucks, vocal reverb throws, filter sweeps on the full mix in the break, sub drop, laser zaps (psy).

## Arrangement
- DJ intro 32 (kick, hats, bass) → break 32 (chords, piano, melody, vocal; drums out at bar 9) → build 16 (snare roll, riser, key lift) → drop 32 (lead + rolling bass + full kit) → break 2 16 → build 8 → drop 2 32 (lead octave up) → outro 32. Crash on every 8/16, fill on bar 8 of each phrase (snare roll or reverse crash).

## Mix signature
- Kick tight and clean, bass offbeat and sidechained (the pump IS the groove), lead wide and bright w/ reverb/delay, pads behind, hats crisp; loud, bright, mono low end.

## Common mistakes
- A bass on the downbeats (not offbeat/rolling), no sidechain pump, a drop w/o a lead, a breakdown w/o a swell/roll, major chords throughout, swing, a kick w/ too much sub tail (keep it tight at 138), no crash/riser transitions.
