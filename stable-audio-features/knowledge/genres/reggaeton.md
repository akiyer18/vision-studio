# Reggaeton / Dembow
slug: reggaeton
aliases: reggaeton, reggaetón, dembow, perreo, bad bunny type beat, latin urban, latin trap (adjacent), urbano, tainy, dem bow
tempo: 88-100 (modern radio 92-96; dembow dominicano 110-120)

## Summary
Reggaeton, 92-96 BPM, straight to slight swing 0.05. The dembow: kick on 1 and 3 (steps 0, 8) w/ snares on the 3-3-2 gallop (steps 3, 6, 11, 14) often as rim+snare layers; plus kick on 0 and 8 reinforced by a short 808. Drums: punchy clean kick, tight snappy snare w/ rim/clap layer, 16th hats light w/ open hats on offbeats, shakers, timbale/cowbell/conga accents, perc fills. Kick: "punchy reggaeton kick, tight 60 Hz thump w/ click, 250 ms, dry". Snare: "tight snappy reggaeton snare layered w/ rimshot, sharp, 150 ms, short room". Pattern: kick 0:120 8:118 (variant 0,6,8) | snare 3:112 6:118 11:112 14:118 | hats 8ths 85/60 + open hat 10 | shaker 16ths 45 | cowbell/timbale 2,10:60. Bass: tuned sine/808 following the kick and the dembow accents (0, 6, 8, 11), short-medium notes, root w/ 5th/b7 moves, slides at phrase ends; Latin trap uses a long 808. Chords: minor keys (Am, Em, Dm, Gm), i-VI-III-VII or i-iv-VI-V, synth pluck/piano/marimba/steel-pan/guitar, pads, horn stabs; reggaeton pluck melody is sync'd w/ the dembow. Form: 4-8 intro (pluck+perc), 16 verse, 8 hook. Avoid: snare on 2 and 4 (that's not dembow), kick 4otf, no 3-3-2 snare pattern.

## Era & tempo
- Dembow riddim (Shabba Ranks "Dem Bow", 1990) → Puerto Rico 1990s-2000s (DJ Playero, Luny Tunes, Daddy Yankee "Gasolina") → 2017-now global (Bad Bunny, J Balvin, Tainy, Ozuna, Karol G). 88-100 BPM; modern often 92-96. Dominican dembow is faster (110-120) with more frantic percussion.

## Pattern archetypes (16 steps, 94 BPM, straight / swing 0.05)
1. Classic dembow: kick 0:120 8:118 | snare 3:112 6:118 11:112 14:118 (rim layer same steps 80) | hats 8ths 85/60 | open hat 10:80 | shaker 16ths 45 | cowbell 2:60 10:60 bar 2.
2. Modern perreo (Bad Bunny/Tainy): kick 0:122 6:95 8:118 (bar2: 0,8,14:90) | snare 3:110 6:116 11:110 14:116 | hats 16ths light 60-40 | clap layer on 6,14:90 | perc fill 12-15 every 4 bars.
3. Latin trap hybrid (85-95): kick 0:120 10:105 | snare 8:127 (half-time) | dembow snare ghosts 3,11:60 | 808 long | hats 16ths w/ rolls.
4. Breakdown: dembow snares alone (no kick) 4 bars, then kick + bass drop.

## Bass
- TYPE: tuned sine/808 sub (short-medium decay 400-700 ms) that follows the kick and the dembow accents; Latin trap = long gliding 808. Register E1-A1. Pattern: 0:110 6:95 8:108 11:90 (root, root, 5th/b7, root) with a slide into bar 2's root; 2-4 notes per bar, locked to the dembow, never four-on-the-floor.
- Live (verified): Sub808Bass, Boom Sub Bass, Hip-Hop Sub Bass, or Simpler with a tuned 808 one-shot (medium decay).

## Harmony & keys
- Minor keys (Am, Em, Dm, Gm): i-VI-III-VII (Am-F-C-G), i-iv-VI-V (Am-Dm-F-E), i-VII-VI-VII; 1 chord per bar, sometimes 2.
- Instruments: synth pluck (the reggaeton "pluck" lead, saw/square with fast decay, delay), piano, marimba/steel pan, nylon/electric guitar plucks (dancehall-style on the offbeats), warm pads, horn/brass stabs, flute/whistle, accordion (occasionally).
- Melody: syncopated pluck riff that interlocks with the dembow (hits on 2, 5, 9, 13 style), 2-4 bars, repeated; countermelody on the hook.

## Hooks & melodic devices
- The pluck riff; a vocal chop tag ("ey", "wuh", "dale"); airhorn/siren; a pre-hook stop (drums out, pluck + 808 slide); octave-up pluck on the hook; perc roll into the hook.

## Textures & FX
- Vocal tags, airhorn, siren, risers (short, 2-4 bars), reverse snare, impact on the hook, delay throws on the pluck, "perreo" ad-libs, güira/shaker loops.

## Arrangement
- Intro 4-8 (pluck + perc, maybe a tag) → verse 16 (full dembow) → pre 4-8 (pluck up, perc build) → hook 8-16 → verse 16 → hook → bridge 8 (dembow alone or half-time) → hook → outro 4. Fills every 4-8 (perc roll on 12-15, snare stutter).

## Mix signature
- Snare/rim bright and forward, kick and 808 tight and loud, pluck wet with delay, hats light, perc wide; mono sub.

## References (verify)
- Daddy Yankee "Gasolina", Bad Bunny "Tití Me Preguntó"/"Yo Perreo Sola", J Balvin "Mi Gente", Ozuna "Taki Taki", Karol G "Provenza", Tainy productions, Luny Tunes "Mayor Que Yo".

## Common mistakes
- Snare on 2 and 4 or kick 4otf (loses the dembow); no rim/snare layering; bass not following the 3-3-2 accents; busy trap hats; major-key happy chords with a straight grid; no pluck riff; no vocal tags/airhorn.
