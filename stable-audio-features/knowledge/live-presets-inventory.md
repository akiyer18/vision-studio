### Notes for the agent
- Drum kits are Drum Racks: load them on a MIDI track and write GM-style notes (36 kick, 38 snare, 42 closed hat ...). Our own Stability one-shots go into Simpler instead; mixing both is fine.
- Chord presets (E-Piano Cmin7 Chord, ENOugh Chord Pad, Floot Aboot Chord, Afterfade Chord, Ion Snow Pad Chord, Clav Delaychord) play a whole chord per key: write single notes.
- Synth Rhythmic presets are tempo-synced arps and gates: hold one long note per bar.
- Effects presets (Risers, Vinyl Siren, Zipper Transport) are transitions: one note at the end of a section.
