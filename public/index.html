    1 · Hear it

    Hum or beatbox the sound in your head, describe it, and Stability AI turns it into a real sample.
    no recording
    Files land in Live's browser under User Library › ADE Hack. Drag the one-shot onto a MIDI track (Simpler) or a Drum Rack pad.

    2 · Pattern

    Describe the groove in words. You get a MIDI clip, and you can audition it here with your new sample.
    use my sample for
    Drag the .mid from Live's browser onto a track with a Drum Rack (GM mapping) or your Simpler (single mapping).

    3 · Melody & Saw

    Hum a melody (or describe one) to get MIDI notes, make the synth sound with Stability AI, then build the whole thing in Live.
    no recording
    creates a Drums track (your drum sample + pattern) and a Saw track (saw sound + melody), sets the tempo and fires both
